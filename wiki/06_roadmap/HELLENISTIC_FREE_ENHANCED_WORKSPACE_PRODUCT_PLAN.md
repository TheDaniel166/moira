## Product constitution

1. The MIT package and self-hosted REST server expose every admitted
   computation without commercial entitlement code.
2. The hosted ladder is `Free < Enhanced < Urania Workspace`; Workspace inherits
   Enhanced. Reuse the existing centralized server-side entitlement model.
3. A subscription buys workflow, persistence, composition, operational scale,
   and practitioner controls. It does not buy hidden doctrine, sources, failure
   reasons, or a more favorable result.
4. Every displayed result identifies its lineage, source, selector/policy,
   engine version, kernel, time/location semantics, derivation, warnings, and
   `not_evaluable` reasons where applicable.
5. Hellenistic, Hermetic, Persian/Medieval, and later Traditional material remain
   visibly distinct. "Traditional" may be an umbrella navigation category, not
   a license to collapse lineages.
6. Source-owned quantities such as admitted dignity weights remain available as
   their named doctrinal objects. Moira does not manufacture a universal
   strength, luck, fate, auspiciousness, or confidence score from them.
7. Timelines juxtapose separately named techniques. They do not silently merge
   techniques into a prediction or promised outcome.
8. Electional work remains bounded and evidence-bearing: inspect a supplied
   moment, compare a finite candidate set, or scan a finite interval under named
   constraints. There is no universal "best date" product.

The commercial principle is: **computation stays open; hosted continuity,
synthesis, and professional workflow may be paid.**

## Market finding

The free-market floor is already high:

- [Astro-Seek](https://horoscopes.astro-seek.com/) provides a wide set of free
  traditional calculators, including profections, Zodiacal Releasing, lots,
  primary directions, distributions, Firdaria, and Decennials. Its principal
  weakness is fragmentation across separate calculators.
- [Astro.com](https://www.astro.com/faq/fq_fh_methods_e.htm) keeps broad core
  calculation available without trying to make every specialist method one
  integrated workflow; paid products emphasize storage and publication.
- [Astro Gold](https://www.astrogold.io/ufaq/astro-gold-ios-subscription-model/)
  offers free natal/transit charts and interpretations, then charges for its
  broader feature set: $7.99 monthly, $39.99 annually, or a $69.99 lifetime
  unlock on iOS at the time of research.
- [Solar Fire V9](https://www.alabe.com/solarfireV9.html) is a $360 practitioner
  desktop product. Its paid value is integration: saved chart files, extensive
  customization, primary directions, profections, dynamic hit lists,
  electional searches, calendars, reports, and export.
- [Traditional-Astrology.com](https://traditional-astrology.com/) offers a real
  free traditional reading and charges a one-time $20 for source-cited synthesis
  and PDF delivery.
- [Astrolium](https://astrolium.com/pricing) currently advertises a free
  foundation and paid personal/practitioner/studio workflow at approximately
  $11/$29/$79 monthly. The higher tiers sell clients, scanners, history,
  reports, API access, alerts, and team workflow. Its pricing and Hellenistic
  feature pages are not fully consistent about which timing tools belong to
  which tier, so they are directional evidence rather than a contract model.

Therefore Moira Free cannot be a token sect badge or one locked profection
result. Moira's opportunity is an integrated, inspectable method: competitors
often provide either disconnected calculators or opaque synthesis. Enhanced
should sell personal continuity. Workspace should sell repeatable practitioner
work, not superior astrological truth.

Prices and advertised capabilities above were checked on 2026-07-21. They are
market observations, not independent validation of competitors' calculations.

## Product experience

### Free: Hellenistic essentials

Create a public `/hellenistic-astrology` experience that answers four useful
questions in one coherent flow:

1. Is this a day or night chart, and what follows directly from that sect basis?
2. What are the visible condition components for the seven classical planets?
3. Where are the four foundational lots, and how were they calculated?
4. What annual profection and current ZR/Decennials periods are active now?

The page must show formulas, named policies, sources, and uncertainty/failure
states. Locked cards may preview personal continuity and practitioner tools, but
must not conceal the method used by visible results.

### Enhanced: personal Traditional study

Enhanced adds continuity around one person's charts:

- Saved full condition dossiers and receptions.
- The existing ten curated lots and saved comparisons.
- Full profection schedules and arbitrary-date lookup.
- Full personal ZR timelines, Fortune/Spirit comparison, and transitions.
- Admitted Decennials L1/L2 timelines.
- An adjacent, correctly labelled Firdaria lane.
- Personal multi-lane timelines, exports, and user-authored reminders.
- Chart-aware explanations that cite the exact evidence they summarize.

Enhanced copy should say **continuity and synthesis**, not "advanced truth."

### Workspace: Hellenistic and Traditional practice workbench

Workspace adds practitioner control and repeatability:

- Client/case organization and historical/BCE workflows.
- Full lot catalog, saved lot sets, networks, and cross-chart comparison.
- Dignity/bounds/face policy laboratory and side-by-side receipts.
- Multi-lot ZR comparison and synchronized profection/ZR/Decennials lanes.
- Full primary-directions instrument with source/lineage filtering.
- Finite Dorothean candidate comparison and bounded electional scans.
- Batch jobs, reusable policy profiles, annotations, imports, exports, report
  templates, and case-level audit history.
- Inspector-aware conversation and memory grounded only in serialized evidence.

Workspace should initially remain one named practitioner seat. Teams,
white-labelling, unlimited automation, and public write API are later commercial
decisions, not launch requirements for a $19 tier.

Do not add entitlements to `moira` or `moira_server`. Hosted commercial gates

The six-gate engine roadmap now owns this receipt. Whole-sign

### Hosted website surface

Recommended composition procedures:

- `hellenistic.catalog` - public lineage, methods, sources, admitted selectors,
  omissions, and capability status.
- `hellenistic.essentials` - public compute for the complete Free snapshot.
- `hellenistic.currentPeriods` - public compute; server freezes one current
  instant.
- `hellenistic.dossier` - Enhanced persisted condition dossier.
- `hellenistic.timeline` - Enhanced personal lanes and arbitrary date/range.
- `hellenistic.export` - Enhanced personal PDF/JSON/CSV/ICS.
- `hellenistic.alerts` - Enhanced user-authored transitions.
- `hellenistic.caseTimeline` - Workspace case composition.
- `hellenistic.compareCandidates` - Enhanced finite manual comparison; Workspace
  adds saved case sets.
- `hellenistic.scanElection` - Workspace-only bounded scan.
- `hellenistic.batch` - Workspace-only bounded batch operation.
- `hellenistic.auditReceipt` - Workspace persistent audit retrieval.

Use existing `publicComputeProcedure`, `protectedEnhancedProcedure`,
`workspaceProcedure`, centralized entitlement helpers, and `LockedFeature` only
as a UI affordance. Server rejection must occur before engine work.

Composition rules:

- A dossier groups evidence; it does not manufacture an overall score.
- A timeline uses separate labelled lanes; it does not output a fate forecast.
- Candidate comparison shows each named predicate as pass, fail, or
  `not_evaluable`.
- Scans require explicit start, end, timezone, step, named constraints, and hard
  duration/candidate/result ceilings.
- Every saved composition records the exact atomic results, policy, engine,
  kernel, and source identities used.

## Explicit non-products and deferrals

Do not offer:

- Universal dignity, condition, luck, fate, auspiciousness, or confidence scores.
- Tier-dependent formulas, doctrine, source visibility, or failure reasons.
- Prognostic certainty, life advice, promised outcomes, or medical/financial
  decision guidance.
- A universal best-election finder or unbounded electional search.
- Hidden selectors, silent fallback, or AI-invented doctrine.
- Firdaria marketed as Hellenistic, or Sahl/Lilly rules marketed as ancient.
- DE441 marketed as spiritually superior or secret premium accuracy.
- Longevity as life-expectancy or death prediction.
- Triacontaeteris without recovered and admitted first-principles doctrine.
- Hephaistio L4, which is not admitted.
- Triplicity doctrine comparison until more than the single admitted Dorothean
  doctrine exists.
- Teams, white-labelling, or unlimited automation as launch requirements.

## Launch measures

Hard targets:

- Zero tier-dependent atomic computation differences under an identical frozen
  request and resource profile.
- Zero lineage, private-data, invented-score, or hidden-selector contract leaks.
- At least 99.5% successful Free essentials requests.
- Free essentials p95 below 3 seconds.
- Enhanced personal timeline p95 below 8 seconds; otherwise use an asynchronous
  job with a durable receipt.
- Workspace scans and batches always obey published hard ceilings and never run
  as unbounded synchronous work.

Privacy-safe product measures may include completed essentials snapshots,
method/source drawer use, saved dossiers, timeline composition, policy switches,
exports, alerts, candidate comparisons, Workspace case/batch use, and conversion
between tiers. These measures may improve workflow and presentation only.
