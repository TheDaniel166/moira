## Numerical discrepancy and evidence classes

The 2013 article's D9 example states advancement of 1 degree 25 minutes,
multiplied by nine, but prints a result of 13 degrees 45 minutes. Independent
sexagesimal arithmetic gives **12 degrees 45 minutes**. That is a source
arithmetic discrepancy, not an engine tolerance problem. Its D24 example
consistently maps half a degree by 24 to 12 degrees and reverses from 30 to 18.
The profile follows the explicit proportional law; it does not reproduce the
defective D9 numeral or silently replace it in the source record.

The textbook's Jupiter input and Sagittarius sign are published paired sign
evidence. Applying the proportional law to its remaining 28 arcminutes yields
28 Sagittarius in D60. **That mapped degree is a derived fixture**, not a
published coordinate in the textbook. Other literal parity/wrap fixtures are
likewise derived, with exact rational arithmetic in tests.

The 2009 article publishes D60 Venus 15 Aquarius 18, Sun 9 Cancer 41, Moon
8 Cancer 06 and Sun 6 Pisces 55 in examples 6, 8 and 9. Their corresponding
natal D1 longitudes are absent from these examples. Birth details, true nodes
and the author's Jagannatha ayanamsa do not constitute paired longitude inputs
or a reproducible oracle for this profile. Those coordinates are contextual
evidence that the author used full D60 positions; they were not inverted into
invented published inputs or replayed under Moira's Lahiri defaults.
