| Aktinobolia (striking with a ray) | Evaluated (since 6.9.8). Direction from the primary texts, not a Schmidt reconstruction: sight is cast forward and the ray backward, so the later planet in zodiacal order strikes the earlier one (Hephaistio I.16, Engelbrecht p. 74; Antiochus/Rhetorius, CCAG I pp. 155-157). Sextile, square and trine strike. The opposition has no side and is a policy gate: `excluded` (default) or `mutual_strike` (the Antiochus/Rhetorius "four figures"). The sources give no degree orb for rays; the 3° default is borrowed from adherence and the moiety mode is Lilly's (1647).
