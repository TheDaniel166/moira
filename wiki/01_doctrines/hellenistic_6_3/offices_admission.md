# Hellenistic Offices Admission

**Verdict: admit only a fail-closed predominator hunt.**
**Never** call `find_hyleg` or `calculate_longevity`.

## Object

Hellenistic offices: predominator (*epikratētōr*), house-master
(*oikodespotēs*). Ties fail closed and preserve candidates.

## Not this object

- Bonatti Hyleg / Alcocoden / Ptolemaic years (`moira/longevity.py`)
- Schmidt “captain of the ship” wizard as default doctrine
- A scored hybrid of Ptolemy III, Valens III, and Dorotheus

If the packet cannot pick one hunt without a scored hybrid, the office is
`not_evaluable` with candidates preserved.
