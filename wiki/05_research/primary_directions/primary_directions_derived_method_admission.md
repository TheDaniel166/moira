# Typed derived significators across primary-direction methods

Admission date: 2026-10-23. Engine-only, staged for the combined release.

## Computational object

Fortune, catalogue lots and an existing selected Ptolemy predominator now
compose with all eight registered methods through the existing typed target
system. The 28 presets retain typed configuration, including their specialized
relations and motions. This is coordinate composition with the current
projection laws, including shared narrow laws; it is not an assertion that
all eight methods have independent historical lineages or that every school
historically directed every catalogue lot.

No formula, projection law, native implementation, time key, target vessel,
public signature, default, root/facade export or REST schema changes. Only
policy admission is widened for explicitly configured typed targets.

directional method neither reruns selection nor changes the source doctrine.

## Preserved boundaries

- Typed configuration remains opt-in. A name alone cannot supply coordinates;
  injected synthetic natal-body entries cannot replace typed construction.
- Fortune and lots remain significators. They do not gain promissor, aspect-
  source, midpoint-source, reflection-source or parallel-source roles.
- A native selected planet/angle retains its ordinary capabilities.
- Catalogue recipes, explicit sect, external-reference context and dependency
  policies remain with their existing owners. Missing or stale source data
  continues to fail explicitly.
- Placidus mundane and Placidian classic do not acquire zodiacal spaces.
- Ordinary direct/traditional-converse, explicit neo-converse, and direct-only
  policies preserve their existing definitions. Meridian retains its existing
  significator-RA-minus-promissor-RA orientation.
- Rapt presets retain their relation-specific direct or converse motion and
  matching ordinary bound chronology; no cross-motion fallback is introduced.
- Signed primary motion remains restricted to the existing Topocentric
  zodiacal branch. Its exact target-policy check now includes the derived
  significator class only when a typed Fortune/lot identity is configured.
  Relation, latitude, perfection, zero-event and antipodal gates are retained.
- Signed-primary-motion timeline bound chronology remains unadmitted. This
  existing restriction is independent of typed target construction.

## Fixed-star composition

An explicitly configured typed derived significator may now receive an already
admitted catalogue-backed fixed-star conjunction. The native planet/angle
classes remain admitted; nodes and house cusps are not added to this branch.
Removing the typed configuration while retaining the derived class does not
grant a star-significator admission. Fixed-star opposition, wider aspects,
epoch/frame reduction and catalogue provenance remain unchanged.

Ptolemaic Fortune, sect-reversed Fortune, Spirit, Basis, and existing Sun, Moon,
Ascendant and Fortune predominator results. Specialized presets use their
actual mundane-aspect, mundane-parallel, rapt, reflected or declination-parallel

equatorial right-ascension limits for all methods with their own orientations;
direct-only and neo-converse policies; fixed-star conjunction gating; missing
unrequested sources; polar-domain boundaries; mundane-only rejection; signed
policy/timeline restrictions; both rapt motions; and all six time keys with
filter-invariant bound partitions for every method.

`test_primary_directions_lots.py` runs every current catalogue lot by day and
night through all eight ordinary mundane method presets: 8,192 construction
comparisons and directional identities. This is owner-composition/regression
evidence. Independent formula and geometry invariants remain separate from
historical authority or universal-school validation. Existing geometry,
native-parity, office, lots and transport suites remain required.

The primary-direction geometry owners and their existing method-specific source
fixtures govern projection. Formula sources and source-binding limits remain in
`primary_directions_derived_points.md`, `primary_directions_catalogue_lots.md`,
and the lots source-verification record. DE441 supplies astronomical inputs and
dynamic-key ephemerides, not historical-doctrine validation.
