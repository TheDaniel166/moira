# Candidate rules and ambiguity register

This register belongs to the [2026-10-22 research packet](README.md).
`R01`-`R30` identify candidate rule families; they are not thirty admitted
runtime predicates. `A01`-`A25` identify decisions or defects that prevent
unqualified promotion. Primary pages are linked through the
[source inventory](SOURCE_INVENTORY.md) and individual records in
[packet.json](packet.json).

## Evidence contract

Keep these stages separate: source located, passage visually checked,
operationally specified, independently fixture-checked, implemented,
validated, admitted. This packet reaches the first two stages for selected
passages. The existing v1 admission is a separate historical contract.

Every future atomic rule needs author/edition and exact passage, proposition,
required inputs, context, preconditions, exceptions, evaluation epoch,
dependencies, precedence, missing-input behavior and independent fixtures.
Every conclusion must cite its supporting and opposing witnesses. No numeric
confidence or universal affirmative/negative score follows from this register.

An epoch is one of: question, last separation, intermediate contact, final
contact, or explicitly unspecified by the source. A source that leaves an
epoch unspecified does not license quietly choosing the initial chart.

## Orb-table receipt (A05)

All entries are **full orbs in degrees**, not already halved moieties.

| Body | First printed set, p.107 | Second printed set, p.107 | Current fixed constant |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Saturn | 10 | 9 | 10 |
| Jupiter | 12 | 9 | 12 |
| Mars | 7.5 | 7 | 7 |
| Sun | 17 | 15 | 15 |
| Venus | 8 | 7 | 7 |
| Mercury | 7 | 7 | 7 |
| Moon | 12.5 | 12 | 12 |

Combined contact range is `(full_orb_A + full_orb_B) / 2`. P.110's
Saturn/Jupiter example halves nine and nine, giving nine degrees; the current
constants give eleven. The current table remains a fixed existing policy.
This packet does not rename it, change it or imply that the source requires
one unqualified replacement.
