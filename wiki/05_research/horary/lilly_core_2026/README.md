## Decision at this checkpoint

The original 1647 photographed pages govern. OCR and modern editorial material
are navigation aids. A second scan of the same edition can resolve a damaged
glyph; it cannot provide independent doctrinal corroboration.

The principal findings are:

- Exact astronomical contact and a successful source-conditioned judgement
  need distinct representations. Lilly qualifies conjunctions, easy aspects,
  squares and oppositions differently.
- Reception has direction, basis, scope and epoch. Lilly's general discussion
  also includes a route without an aspect; the current six-form classifier
  does not make that route available.
- Moon/VOC qualifications, contextual cautions, same-sign combustion and
  turning instructions need explicit treatment before a broader profile can
  be admitted.
- Source language leaves genuine choices: alternative orb tables, the meaning
  of application within a sign, relative planetary weight, and reception at
  different moments. These remain visible in the ambiguity register.
- The catalogue inventories **36 Book II case groups using 35 distinct printed
  figures**. The p.456 office question reuses p.455; p.473 is an event/inception
  example. Book I contributes one further printed figure and two additional
  erection exercises without new figures. These are different denominators.

No new rule or case in this packet is admitted as an executable or predictive
golden. That is an admission result, not a missing status field. Visual heading
and diagram checks do not constitute double transcription of every coordinate,
complete judgement analysis, or independent confirmation of reported outcomes.

## Finite core and deliberate deferrals

The core covers chart/time provenance, house and role foundations, the named
dignity table, application/reception and solar condition, considerations,
Moon/VOC, completion/blocking mechanisms, and qualitative timing prerequisites.
It includes the general house significations as a source map, not twelve
implemented topic modules.

The complete planetary/sign description chapters, all topic-specific clauses,
antiscia, lots, fixed-star conditions, symbolic timing tables and every
unillustrated narrative example require later source tranches. The fortitude
table is inventoried as a dependency; no universal judgement score is admitted.
Book III is auxiliary for house-policy context, not a way to import natal
doctrine into questions. Other Western authors need separate packets.
