## Fixture promotion contract

Each case must earn its own promotion; catalogue presence grants none of the
following gates automatically:

1. **Printed-chart transcription:** two independent entries of every used
   planet, degree/minute, sign, motion mark and cusp, with page/crop locators,
   uncertain readings and alternate-image resolution. Keep printed values.
2. **Historical input receipt:** literal and normalized dates, calendar/year
   start/noon clock, timezone or local-time model, locality and coordinates,
   conversion derivation and uncertainty. Unknown time stays unknown. C31
   needs its own offset receipt even though it shares a printed figure.
3. **Question/role receipt:** literal question location, normalized proposition,
   entities, caller context, radical/turned derivation, each proposed
   significator and ambiguity. Chapter placement cannot replace the actual
   house assignment in the judgement.
4. **Doctrinal witness:** passage-by-passage support and opposition, source
   rules, event order and conditions, qualitative judgement and any timing
   derivation. Mark hypothetical extensions and conjectures explicitly.
5. **Numerical reconstruction:** a separate modern calculation with declared
   astronomical products, kernels, house policy, timescales and tolerances.
   Preserve disagreements with the printed chart and explain them; never
   edit the printed data to manufacture agreement.
6. **Outcome receipt:** distinguish what Lilly reports, when it was known,
   independently dated corroboration and what remains unknown. C35 cannot
   demonstrate a prediction of release after the question. An author's
   retrospective report alone is not independent outcome verification.
7. **Test admission:** source-owned expected results, reviewer provenance,
   positive/negative/boundary controls and explicit assertions about which
   evidence class the fixture validates. No implementation-output goldens.

Recommended first tranche: E01-E03 and D01-D08, then C28 for the existing role
assignment lineage, C08 for a transaction and C13/C14 for marriage. Select a
complete topic module only after its full rule chapter and worked judgement
are collated. Numerical agreement, faithful historical interpretation and
prospective predictive performance must remain separately measured.
