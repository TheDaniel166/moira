## Summary by question (details and quotations in the page entries below)

1. **Translation (P05/P06).** One worked translation: **C08, p.220**, the ☽ "tranſlating the influence of ♂ ... to ♄" (☽ separating from ☌ ♂, applying □ ♄; ♂ and ♄ are co-significators by dignity in the 7th and the ascendant). Lilly mentions **none** of the P06 conditions: no prior separation of ♂ and ♄, no reception of the ☽ by ♂, no check of intervening contacts, no heaviness. The second leg is a **square**, which gives delay ("with some leisure and slowly"). Doctrine pages list translation as a bare alternative to aspect (pp.217, 222, 225, 226, 228, 235), twice with a "later / after some space of time" effect (pp.222-223, 225), and p.235 makes the translator's house rulership describe the matter. p.229 uses "translated" loosely for one planet committing its disposition to another. Verdict for this range: **structural use (P05)**; no support for the P06 conditions.
2. **Prohibition (B01/B02).** Not named in this range. p.236-237 (messengers): an application to an infortune by □/☍ *before* the separation from the 7th lord = impediment in the business; *after* it = delays in returning. This is an ordering rule, not a prohibition definition. Non-finding for one-vs-both and for swiftness.
3. **Refranation / frustration (B03/B04).** **C08 p.220:** ♀ "applying to a △ of ♄ ... had no abſciſſion or fruſtation ere the perfect aſpect". So Lilly tests for frustration/abscission **on a trine**, which is evidence against the conjunction-only reading of B04. In C08, ♄ (the heavier principal) is retrograde, and Lilly reads this as both significators "strongly applying", not as refranation (p.220). Retrograde significators are otherwise read as affliction, or as delay until they turn direct (pp.221, 237, 239). Refranation is not named in this range.
4. **Heaviness (H01).** "Ponderous" is never defined. p.242: "♄ ... is a ponderous Planet, and of ſlow motion" (two properties named together). p.225: a collector "more ponderous then themſelves ... ſhall be the receiver of their diſpoſition". p.229: "the ponderous Planet to whom the Lord of the aſcendant commits his Diſpoſition" (the receiver is the planet applied to). p.238: application to a ponderous planet "ſhewes more certainly the day". Retrogradation: p.223 counts it as an affliction of the receiver, and p.220 shows a retrograde heavier planet still treated as the one applied to. Nothing here decides between `traditional_order` and `instantaneous_speed`.
5. **Collection (P07).** p.225 (doctrine): the collector must be **more ponderous than** the significators. It is the receiver of their disposition, and its condition decides the outcome (p.226). No "do not behold" condition is stated; collection is the last fallback, "if none of these be". **C09 p.239:** "any collection of light from ☿ to ♄, and that Planet ſo collecting had received ♄ or ☿". The **collector receives** (one principal is enough), which is the opposite direction from P07's "each principal receives the collector". Also, ☿ and ♄ are in applying ☍ there, so collection is offered even though the principals do behold each other.
6. **Good houses (Q01).** Two lists, each tied to its context: **p.229** "a good houſe, viz. in the ſecond, eleventh or ninth" (for the ponderous receiver, set against cadent); **p.234** "the tenth, eleventh, fift, third, ninth, firſt or ſecond houſes" (where application plus reception gives "Love and Unity"). pp.224, 225 and 227 use "good house" without defining it, set against cadent or the 8th. p.227 also gives a second sense of "cadent": a planet's position counted from its own sign ("ever a Planet is Angular in any of his owne Houſes").
7. **Reception overriding (P08).** p.217: mutual reception is listed as an alternative route ("or if there be mutuall reception or tranſlation, or collation"). p.237: reception with "□ or ☍ ... yet he was well received; but ... framed ſome excuſe", so reception makes a hard aspect succeed, with a flaw left in the outcome. p.220 (C08): being received by ☉ in exaltation is "an argument I ſhould proceed ... notwithſtanding ♀ her many Debilities". p.239 (C09): reception would only have made Lilly "not ... ſo peremptory". p.227: reception counts only when essential, since accidental dignity gives "hopes, but not reall aſſurance". Nothing here says reception overrides prohibition or refranation.
8. **Conjunction / malevolent aspect (P01/B05).** p.222: "☌ is not properly an aſpect", though it is still listed with ✶ △ □. p.217: "the ☌ of both Significators beſt of all performes"; □/☍ give "difficulty and much labour". p.226: "malevolent aſpect of the Infortunes" (♄ and ♂, per the margin). p.227: "any evill aſpect of the Lord of the ſixth, eighth or twelfth, be he what Planet he will ... if he have □ or ☍". Taken together, "malevolent/evil aspect" here means a □ or ☍ from a malefic or from the lord of an evil house. Interp.: this argues against both pure readings of B05.
9. **Route precedence / perfection.** p.225 gives an explicit **fallback order** for children: joining of the significators, then translation from the 5th lord to the 1st lord ("after some space of time"), then placement, then collection by a heavier planet. pp.222-223 rank direct application before translation/collection ("not so soone"). p.217 ranks aspect types (☌ best; □/☍ laborious). C08 (pp.220-221) stacks several routes (♀ △ ♄ application, ☉ △ ♄, lunar translation, reception) and times the outcome by the principals' later ☌ (♀ to ☉, about 6°, about 6 weeks; bargain struck on the day of the ☌). Judgement is otherwise by the plurality of testimonies (pp.225, 232, 241). p.240: a nativity denying children outranks a horary.

Non-findings: pp.213-216 (remove/stay, water-works, the nature of treasure), pp.230-233 (sex, twins, time of birth, abortion), p.241 (C10 sex count) contain no route doctrine beyond the notes above.

## Fifth-house doctrine and cases, pp.222-242

(Fourth-house pages 213-221 follow after p.242.)

### p.222 (doctrine; fifth house, "Whether they shall ever have any Child")
- Quote: "if there be rendring of vertue or light one unto another, or tranſlation or collection by or from the prin-cipall Significators, you may ſtill continue your judgment, that the Querent ſhall have iſſue, but not ſo ſoone" (runs onto p.223).
- Paraphrase: if neither the Lord of the Ascendant nor the Moon applies to the Lord of the 5th, a rendering of virtue, a translation or a collection between the principal significators still gives a yes, but later than a direct application would.
- Answers Q1/Q5/Q9: translation and collection are named as **substitute routes** when direct application is absent, with **a delay effect** ("not so soon"). No conditions (separation, reception, heaviness) are stated here. Also: Lilly lists ☌ ✶ △ □ as the aspects and adds "though ☌ is not properly an aspect" (Q8 terminology).
- Also p.222: Lord of the 5th in the Ascendant, or Lord of the Ascendant / Moon in the 5th, is "a strong argument" (placement, not aspect, as testimony).

### p.223 (doctrine; same question, continued)
- Quote: "have reſpect to that Planet who is receiver of the Diſpoſition of the Significators; who if he be cleer from misfortune ... viz. from Retrogradation, Combuſtion, or Cadency in Houſe, it gives great hopes of iſſue."
- Paraphrase: look at the planet that "receives the disposition" of the significators (i.e. the planet they apply to / commit to); if that receiver is free of retrogradation, combustion and cadency, hopes are good.
- Answers Q4/Q5: "receiver of the disposition" is the planet to which the significators commit; the receiver's own condition matters. Retrogradation is listed as an affliction of the receiver.
- Quote: "If no Aſpect happen betwixt them, its not then likely he will have any; and yet ſome ſay ... if the Planet to whom the ☽ applies be in an Angle, the Querent may have Iſſue."
- Paraphrase: the planet the Moon separates from and the one it applies to: if they aspect each other, children; if no aspect between them, unlikely, though "some say" an angular application-target suffices. Interp.: this is a Moon-mediated linking test (the Moon's separation-from / application-to pair must themselves be in aspect), not called translation.
- "If a Woman ask, whether she may conceive": testimonies are placements (Lord of Asc in 7th, Lord of 5th in 1st, etc.) and good/bad planets in angles; "If good and bad Planets be mixed together, she may perhaps conceive ... but they will not live". No route doctrine.

### p.224 (doctrine; timing; "Whether the Querent shall have Children")
- Timing by house of the Lord of the 5th (1st = first year, 2nd = second, 10th third, 7th fourth, 4th fifth); moveable signs hasten, common slower, fixed prolong.
- Quote: "if he be ſwift in motion and direct, he doth make the more haſte in performance of the buſineſſe".
- Q4 relevance: speed/direction affects **timing**, not route validity.
- Quote (Q6): "If the Lord of the aſcendant be in the fourth or ſeventh, and ♃ in a good Houſe of heaven, doe you ſay, the party ſhall have a Childe a long time after". Contrast: "the Lord of the Aſcendant in an evill place or Houſe of heaven, and ♃ Cadent, or in the eight". No list of good houses given here; "good house" opposed to cadent / eighth.

### p.225 (doctrine; children; "If a Man shall have Children by his Wife")
- Quote (Q6): "the more Children if ♃ ♀ or ☉ be in ✶ or △ with him out of good houſes".
- Paraphrase: an infortune in the 5th, if direct, swift, oriental and dignified, signifies children; more so if ♃/♀/☉ cast a sextile or trine to it "out of good houses". Again no definition of which houses are good.
- Quote: "if he be angular with reception of the Lord of that Angle where he is, or in the eleventh or fift with the like reception, it is a ſure teſtimony". (Reception by the house-lord as a strengthening condition; Q7 partial.)
- "ſeldome conclude without two teſtimonies" (judgment is by accumulation of testimonies).
- **Key passage (Q4/Q5):** "then ſee if any tranſlation be from the Lord of the fift to the Lord of the aſcendant; that's an argument of having Children after ſome ſpace of time ... if none of theſe be, conſider if the Lord of the aſcendant, the ☽, and Lord of the fift be not joyned to a Planet more ponderous then them-ſelves; for he collecting both their lights, ſhall be the recei-ver of their diſpoſition, and ſhall ſignifie whether the Childe or Children (if any be) ſhall live or not" (to p.226).
- Paraphrase: order of tests: (1) Lord of Asc or Moon joined to Lord of 5th = yes; (2) else translation from Lord of 5th to Lord of Asc = yes, after some time; (3) placement tests; (4) else a planet "more ponderous" than the significators to which they are all joined collects their lights and is "the receiver of their disposition"; its condition (p.226: impedited or not, retrograde, combust, peregrine) decides whether the children live.
- Answers: Q5 collector must be **more ponderous than** the significators (heavier than both), and the collector **is the receiver of their disposition** (they commit to it; the receiving runs from collector side as "receiver", no essential-dignity reception stated). No "do not behold" condition is stated here; it is introduced as a fallback "if none of these be". Q1: translation direction stated (from Lord of 5th to Lord of Asc) with no conditions; effect = "after some space of time" (delay). Q4: "ponderous" is not defined here. Q9: explicit **fallback ordering** among direct joining, translation, placement, collection.

### p.226 (doctrine; children / "Whether she is with Child")
- Collector's condition: "if he be not impedited the Children then ſhall live, but if he be Retrograde, Combuſt, Peregrine, or otherwiſe unfortunate, neither will the Children live long".
- ♀ in the 5th impedited: "it will not come to perfection, for the Woman ſhall ſuffer abortion" (perfection used for pregnancy coming to term, not an astrological route term).
- Square of infortunes to the 5th "ſeems to hinder conception"; "the two former malevolents caſt their ☍ to the fifth". Q8: "malevolent aspect" wording at the bottom: "the Lord of the aſcendant and the ☽ be in the fifth houſe free from the malevolent aſpect of the Infortunes". Interp.: here "malevolent aspect" is an aspect **of the infortunes**; margin note says "I meane ♄ and ♂ for Planets, not ☋", and the text adds that the Lord of the 6th, 8th or 12th's evil aspect counts too (p.227). Bears on B05 reading: "malevolent aspect" = aspect from malefic planets (and from lords of evil houses), specifically □ or ☍ (p.227).
- "If the Lord of the aſcendant or ☽ behold the Lord of the fifth with any aſpect or tranſlation" (testimony of pregnancy): translation treated alongside aspect as a way of "beholding" (Q1 structural use).

### p.227 (doctrine; "Whether she is with Child" continued)
- Quote (B05/Q8): "take any evill aſpect of the Lord of the ſixth, eighth or twelfth, be he what Planet he will, to be an affliction, if he have □ or ☍ to the Lord of the fifth, or Lord of the aſcendant, or the ☽."
- Interp.: Lilly here makes affliction depend on (a) the **aspect type** (□/☍) and (b) the afflicter being lord of an evil house, "be he what Planet he will", i.e. not only ♄/♂. Supports a hybrid reading for "malevolent aspect": square/opposition from malefics or evil-house lords.
- Quote (Q7): "The Lord of the aſcendant, or Lord of the fifth houſe aſpecting a Planet in an Angle with reception, and rendring up his vertue unto him; if the ☽ be in reception with any Planet in an Angle, that is, eſſentially Fortified, elſe not; for accidentall dignities in this manner of judgement, giveth hopes, but not reall aſſurance."
- Paraphrase: reception must be by essential dignity; accidental dignity alone gives hope, not assurance.
- Quote (reception definition by dignities, Q1/Q5/Q7): "The Lord of the Aſcendant received in either Houſe, Triplicity or Exaltation, and the receiver of the Lord of the Aſcendant having alike dignity in the Houſe, Triplicity, Exaltation, or Term of the received". Interp.: mutual reception by house/triplicity/exaltation/term listed as a testimony (only a testimony of pregnancy; no override claim).
- Q6 (good house; amicable aspect): "If the Lord of the Aſcendant behold the Aſcendant with an amicable aſpect, out of any good Houſe". Again no list.
- Cadent from own house: Lilly defines a planet's house-position relative to its own sign: "for ever a Planet is Angular in any of his owne Houſes" (Mars in Aries = angular, Taurus = succedent, Gemini = cadent "as from his owne house"). This is a second notion of "cadent" (from own house/exaltation) relevant to how "cadent" may be read.

### p.228 (doctrine)
- Quote (Q1): "The Lord of the aſcendant joyned to a Retrograde Planet, or one in a Cadent Houſe, or received by a Retrograde or Combuſt Planet, no aſpect or tranſlation of light betwixt the Lord of the fifth and Lord of the aſcendant; judge by the major teſtimonies."
- Paraphrase: negative testimonies include no aspect **or translation** between the two principals; translation again treated as the alternative to aspect.
- "If the Lord of the fifth behold a Planet in an Angle with reception" (testimony she is with child).
- "if other ſignificators be more prevalent then they" (testimony weighing).

### p.229 (doctrine; Chap. XL "Whether a Woman be with Child")
- Quote (Q1/Q4/Q6): "if the Lord of the aſcendant his vertue or diſpoſition be tranſlated to any Planet in an Angle, the more certaine you may judge; if he to whom the Lord of the aſcendant commits his Diſpoſition, be received of the Lord of the aſcendant, or the Lord of the aſcendant by him".
- Interp.: here "translated" means the Lord of the Asc **commits** (applies/renders) its disposition to another planet: a looser sense of "translation" (= rendering of virtue to one planet), not the three-body route. Reception in either direction counts.
- Quote (Q4/Q6, key): "but if the ponderous Planet to whom the Lord of the aſcendant commits his Diſpoſition be in a good houſe, viz. in the ſecond, eleventh or ninth, not in ☌ with the Infortunes, and the ☽ be free, it notes the Conception ſhall come to a good end".
- **Q6 finding:** an explicit (context-specific) list of "good house": **second, eleventh or ninth**, given as the alternative to the cadent house mentioned just before ("be in a cadent house, it notes the Woman hath taken griefe"). Interp.: since the 2nd is succedent, the 9th and 11th are cadent/succedent good houses, the list appears to be "good non-angular houses" (the angles are covered elsewhere). It is not a general definition.
- Q4: "ponderous Planet" again = the planet receiving the Lord of Ascendant's disposition (heavier, the one applied to). No definition of ponderous.

### p.230 (doctrine; twins, sex). No P4-relevant material.
- Sex rules by sign gender; ☿ masculine or feminine "as he is in aſpect or ☌ with a Maſculine or Feminine Planet" (p.230-231), ☿ oriental of ☉ masculine. Non-finding for routes.

### p.231 (doctrine; how long conceived; time of birth)
- "fee which of all theſe is neereſt from the ſeparation of any Planet ... and from what manner of aſpect this ſeparation is" (separating aspect gives months since conception; ☌ = one month etc.). Uses separation for past-time only.
- Time of birth: "When ♂ and ☉ are in ☌ with the Lord of the fifth, and with the ☽ and Lord of the houre, or the more part of them". Part of Children directed. Non-finding for route conditions.

### p.232 (doctrine; birth by day/night). Majority-of-testimonies method ("the major part of thoſe Significators"; if significators disagree, "take him that is Eſſentially moſt ſtrong"). Q9: judgement by plurality/ strongest; no route precedence. Non-finding otherwise.

### p.233 (doctrine; abortion, state of the mother, infant). Non-finding for routes. Applying/separating bad aspects of the Lords of 8th/4th; "the ☽ fortunately applying to either of the Fortunes, or to the ☉, or indeed to any good aſpect of the Infortunes" (good aspects of infortunes acceptable).

### p.234 (doctrine; "Whether Unity is like to be betwixt the Infant and the Parent")
- Quote (Q6/Q7): "if you find reception and application betwixt the Lord of the fift, and Lord of the aſcendant, and this in the tenth, eleventh, fift, third, ninth, firſt or ſecond houſes, there will be Love and Unity".
- **Q6 finding:** a second explicit list of acceptable houses: **10, 11, 5, 3, 9, 1, 2** (i.e. all except 4, 6, 7, 8, 12). Interp.: this is close to the classical "good houses" list (angles 1/10, succedents 2/5/11, cadents 3/9; omitting 4 and 7 among angles here because the context is family harmony, and omitting 6/8/12). Context-specific; not stated as a general definition.
- Requires **both** reception and application.

### p.235 (doctrine; Chap. XLI "Of Embassadours or Messengers")
- Quote (Q1/Q5/Q7): "If you find the application is from a Fortune by a □ or ☍ or ☌, and if there be reception betwixt them, or collection or tranſlation of light by any Planet, and that Planet be either Lord of the tenth, or in the tenth, you may ſay, the cauſe of his Embaſſage is unto the King".
- "If the Planet who is received, or who collects or tranſlates the vertue of one to another, be Lord of the eleventh, he comes to renew the League of Friendſhip".
- Paraphrase: reception, collection and translation are listed as alternatives; the house of the translating/collecting planet **describes** the matter (Q1: translator's house rulership describes the agent/purpose, matching P06's effect clause). No conditions on the translator.
- Q8: "♂ have any malicious aſpects to them both, or to either of them" (malefic aspects; no aspect type given).

### p.236 (doctrine; messenger sent for money)
- Quote (Q1/Q9, separation semantics): "If you find the Lord of the fifth ſeparated from the Lord of the ſeventh, and applying to the Lord of the aſcendant, you may judge the Meſſenger hath effected the thing he went about, is departed from him, and returning home".
- Interp.: this is the structural shape of a translation (5th lord separates from 7th lord, applies to 1st lord), but Lilly reads it as narrative (messenger went and returned), not called "translation" and with no reception/heaviness condition. The house of the planet separated from describes the answer brought.
- Quote (B01/B02-like): "If the Significator of the Meſſenger doe apply by □ or ☍ to an Infortune, before he is ſeparated from the Lord of the ſeventh ... hath had ſome impediment in effecting his Buſineſſe" (to p.237). Chronology (before/after the separation) changes where the impediment falls; Interp.: this is an impediment-by-aspect timing rule, not called prohibition.

### p.237 (doctrine; messengers continued)
- "but if this application to an Infortune happen after that the Lord of the fift was ſeparated from the Lord of the ſeventh, the Meſſenger will have delayes or misfortune in his returning".
- **Q7 key quote:** "If there be reception between the Lord of the fift and ſeventh, and any amicable aſpect, your Meſſenger was well received ... yea though the application be by □ or ☍, yet he was well received; but the party ſought after, framed ſome excuſe".
- Paraphrase: reception makes even a square or opposition application give a good reception, but with a reservation (excuse/defence). Interp.: reception mitigates a hard aspect (it does not cancel it: the outcome still carries a flaw). Does not address prohibition/refranation.
- Timing: "Behold if the Lord of the fift be receded from the Lord of the ſeventh, or applying to the Planet who is his Diſpoſitor"; retrograde significator: messenger returns "when he comes to be Direct". Q3: retrogradation used for timing (return when direct), not as refranation.

### p.238 (doctrine end, then C09 figure)
- Quote (Q4): "the application of the Significator to a ponderous Planet, ſhewes more certainly the day".
- Interp.: "ponderous" = slow/heavy planet; application to it gives more reliable timing. No definition.
- C09 figure (11 June 1635, 2:30 P.M.): ♍ ascending; ☿ in ♊ (Lord of Asc) with ♂; ♄ 29°24 ♐ Rx (Lord of 5th ♑ and 6th ♒); centre "☽ a □ ♄ ad □ ☉" (Moon separating from □ ♄, applying to □ ☉). Chap. XLII heading starts.

### p.239 (C09; "If the Querent should ever have Children", judgement)
- Lilly states: ♄ Lord of the 5th retrograde in ♐; ☿ Lord of the Asc in ♊; both in signs rather barren; ☽ in terms of ♂ in □ of ♄; ☿ afflicted by ♂'s presence "and going to ☍ of ♄"; ☋ in Ascendant; angles afflicted. Judgement: never conceived and never would.
- **Key quote (Q5/Q7):** "Had I found ♃ either fortunating the cuſp of the fift houſe, or in any aſpect to the Lord of the aſcendant, or unto ♄, or if any reception had been betwixt ♄ and ♃, or ♃ and ☿, or any collection of light from ☿ to ♄, and that Planet ſo collecting had received ♄ or ☿, I would not have been ſo peremptory".
- Paraphrase: a collection of light between ☿ and ♄ **with the collector receiving ♄ or ☿** would have softened the negative judgement.
- Answers Q5: reception condition on collection runs **collector receives a principal** (collector in-dignified over the significator: "that Planet so collecting had received ♄ or ☿"), and **only one** principal needs to be received ("♄ or ☿"). This is the reverse direction from S4-17 as summarised in P07 ("each principal receives the collector"). Note the principals here (☿ in ♊ going to ☍ ♄ in ♐) **do** behold each other (applying opposition): so collection is cited as a possible route even where the principals have an (adverse) aspect: Interp.: "do not behold" is not applied here as a precondition; the collection is offered as a mitigating alternative to a bad opposition. Also Q2/Q8: Lilly treats the applying ☍ ☿-♄ as itself adverse (judgement negative), not as perfection (P04 conditions absent: no reception).
- Q3: ♄ (Lord of 5th) retrograde is read as affliction, not as refranation (☿ applies to ♄; ♄ is retrograde, i.e. both move toward each other). Interp.: no refranation is invoked.
- Also Q7: reception is named among things that would have changed the verdict, but only as "would not have been so peremptory" (softening), not as override.

### p.240 (C09 coda; C10 figure)
- "if the Radix affirme Barrenneſſe, it's impoſſible any promiſing Horary Queſtion can contradict its ſignification" (Q9: nativity outranks horary).
- C10 figure (7 April 1645, 2:15 P.M.): ♍ 8°50 ascending; ☽ 9°50 ♑ in 5th; ♄ 24°37 ♈ (8th) with ☿ 11°00 ♈ and ♀ (in ♈/♉ region with ♄); ☉ 27°48 ♈; ♂ near MC ♊ 2°37 (?); centre "☽ a □ ♀ ad □ ☿" (Moon separating from □ ♀, applying to □ ☿). Dns diei ♂ (?), hor. ☽ (glyphs partly unclear; not needed).

### p.241 (C10 judgement, Chap. XLIII). Sex by plurality of testimonies: 8 masculine vs 4 feminine; "and ſo it proved". Includes "☿ Applying to ♂ his □, and ♂ a Planet [Masculine]". Non-finding for routes; Q9: plurality method.

### p.242 (C10 timing)
- **Key quote (Q4):** "but becauſe ♄ Lord of the fift is a ponderous Planet, and of ſlow motion, I much valued him in this Judg-ment".
- Paraphrase: Lilly gives weight to ♄ in timing because it is ponderous **and** slow. Interp.: "ponderous" and "slow motion" are named together as two properties; ♄ is retrograde-free here (direct in ♈? the figure gives no Rx mark). No statement that retrogradation alters ponderousness.
- Timing method: ☽ to □ ♄ = 14°47'; ☿ to ☌ ♄ = 13°37'; difference ~1°10'; one week per degree, ~14 weeks; delivered 11 July following. Uses applying ☌ ☿-♄ (Lord of Asc to Lord of 5th, bodily) and ☽ to □ ♄ as **timing** measures, not perfection tests. Moon in sign of 5th.
- Q1/Q2: ☿ (Lord of Asc) applies to ♄ (Lord of 5th) bodily in ♈; ☉ at 27°48 ♈ beyond ♄... no prohibition is discussed. Non-finding for prohibition.

### p.214 (Chap. XXXVI, turning rivers / conduits)
- Quote (Q7): "if the ☽ apply to that Planet who is Lord of that Signe wherein ſhe is, and he receive her in any of his Dignities; and if that Planet who is receiver of the ☽ be a Fortune ...".
- "Receiver" = the dispositor to which the Moon applies and which receives her "in any of his Dignities" (reception by any essential dignity; receiver = applied-to planet). Strengthening testimony, no route claim.

### p.217 (doctrine; "If the Querent shall obtaine the Treasure hid")
- **Quote (Q1/Q5/Q8/Q9):** "If the Planet who ſignifies the Treaſure ... doth apply to the Lord of the aſcendant, or if there be mutuall reception or tranſlation, or collation of light and nature betwixt them, it's probable the Querent ſhall obtaine the matter ſought after; if the aſpect be by □ or ☍, then not without difficulty and much labour; the ☌ of both Significators beſt of all performes the buſineſſe".
- Paraphrase: application, mutual reception, translation and "collation" (collection) of light are listed as **co-equal alternatives** ("or ... or"), with no conditions. Hard aspects perfect with difficulty; the conjunction is the best route.
- Q7: mutual reception is listed as a standalone route alongside aspects (consistent with P08 as its own route; nothing about overriding blockers). Q8: conjunction is named a mode of performing ("best of all"); □/☍ perfect "not without difficulty". Q9: a qualitative ranking of aspect routes (☌ best; □/☍ laborious), but no precedence among application / reception / translation / collection.
- Q6-adjacent: "if neither of them be in the aſcendant, or behold it, but be both in cadent houſes, there remaines little hopes".

### p.218 (doctrine; treasure, citing Alkindus)

### p.220 (C08 judgement) - KEY CASE PAGE
- Lilly states: ♀ (Lord of the Asc) for himself; ☉ in the 7th for the seller; "the ☉ receives ♀ in his Exaltation"; ♀ near the 7th cusp.
- **Q7:** "finding ... my Significator received of ☉, and ſo neer to the cuſp of the Angle of the Weſt, it was an argument I ſhould proceed further in the matter, notwithſtanding ♀ her many Debilities". Reception (single, by exaltation, of the querent's significator by the quesited's) outweighs the received planet's debilities as an argument. No aspect-override claim.
- **Q3 (B04) KEY:** "♀ was applying to a △ of ♄, Lord of the fourth, viz. the houſes enquired after, and had no abſciſſion or fruſtation ere the perfect aſpect; a maine ſtrong argument that I ſhould buy the houſes".
  - Lilly checks for "abscission or frustration" **on a trine application**, not a conjunction. Interp.: in practice Lilly applies frustration (abscission) to aspect perfection too, contrary to the conjunction-only reading of B04. He does not say which planet would frustrate. This is case evidence for generalising B04 to aspects (or at least that Lilly checks for it before an aspect perfects).
  - P02 shape: sextile/trine application = "a maine strong argument".
- **Q4 / Q3 KEY:** "and indeed both Significators ſtrongly applyed to a △ aſpect, viz. ♄ and ♀, for ♄ is Retrograde".
  - Interp.: the heavier ♄, being retrograde, moves back toward ♀, so the application is **mutual**; Lilly reads this as strengthening the application ("strongly applied"), not as refranation and not as reversing who is "heavier". Retrogradation of the heavier principal here is not an obstacle. (No explicit statement of which is the "receiver" or the "heavier".)
- ☉ "in perfect △ with ♄"; ☉ Lord of the 11th (hopes) and ♄ Lord of the 4th: "the Lord of the eleventh and he applying unto each other by a △, argued, aſſuredly I ſhould proceed further ... and in the end conclude for them". ♄ also signifies the querent "becauſe he beholds the aſcendant, and therein hath exaltation" (a planet exalted in the ascending sign that aspects the ascendant acts as co-significator of the querent).
- **Q1 KEY (translation in a case):** "The ☽ in the next place tranſlating the influence of ♂, who hath Dignities in the ſeventh, to ♄; having vertue in the aſcendant, though by a □ aſpect (yet out of Signes of long aſcenſions) did much facilitate the matter, and argued my going on, and probability of contracting, but with ſome leiſure and ſlowly, becauſe of the □ aſpect".
  - Lilly states: the Moon translates from ♂ (co-significator of the seller by dignity in the 7th: ♂ rules ♈ on the 7th) to ♄ (co-significator of the querent by exaltation in ♎). The Moon has just separated from the ☌ of ♂ and applies to □ ♄ (centre of the figure).
  - Not mentioned: whether ♂ and ♄ had separated from each other; any reception of the Moon by ♂; any check of intervening contacts; heaviness (Moon is trivially lightest).
  - Interp.: (a) translation is used with **co-significators** (lords of dignities in the relevant houses), not only the house lords; (b) the second leg may be a **square**, with the square softened "out of Signes of long ascensions"; the square produces delay ("with some leisure and slowly"), and the Moon's and ♀'s affliction produces difficulty ("much to doe, and many meetings"). (c) Translation is an additional, facilitating testimony beside the direct ♀-△-♄ application: Lilly stacks routes (direct trine + translation + ☉-△-♄), no precedence. Supports P05 structural reading; P06's extra conditions are not invoked.
- Q6/Q9: "♃ is cadent, and in detriment" (to p.221): the jovial rival's significator (☉ near ✶ ♃) failed because ♃ cadent and in detriment.

### p.221 (C08 judgement, conclusion)
- Correction to the p.219 figure reading: Lilly states "the ☽ in ♍ afflicted by ♂", so the Moon (and ♂) are in ♍ (12th side), not ♌.
- "♀ angular and in aſpect with ♄ Lord of the thing ſought after".
- Q9 (timing/what perfects): "When ♀ and ☉ came to ☌ in ♉, that day I bargained, viz. die ♀ 25. April following; the ſeventeenth of May ♀ and ☽ in ☌; I paid in 530 l. ... So that as ♀ wanted ſix degrees of the body of the ☉, ſo was it ſix weekes and ſome dayes from the time of the Queſtion ere I perfected what the Figure promiſed".
  - Interp.: the outcome was timed by the **bodily conjunction of the two principals** (♀ querent, ☉ seller), ~6° = ~6 weeks, while the yes-judgement was built from receptions, the ♀-△-♄ application, and the Moon's translation. Lilly does not test this ☌ for prohibition in the text.
- Q4/Q3: "♂ Lord of my ſecond houſe Retrograde, argued I ſhould get none of my owne Monies ... nor did I"; money came when ♂ "became direct, which he did twelve dayes after". Retrogradation = denial until station direct (timing), not refranation.
- "♃ ... in ✶ platick with ♀" (platic aspect counted). "♄ Lord thereof [4th], who having no materiall debilities, except Retrogradation and Cadency".
- Margin: rival "endeavour to hinder me" (p.220-221): a jovial man (♃, ☉ near ✶ ♃) tried to buy; failed because ♃ cadent and in detriment. Interp.: a third planet's aspect to the seller's significator read as a rival, not formally a prohibition.
