## Summary by question (pages; "Interp." marks my reading)

No worked case falls in pp.298-332 or 358-362; all findings are doctrine.
Content: general aphorisms (pp.298-302), Chap. XLIX marriage (pp.302-319),
Chap. L fugitives/strays/theft (pp.319-332), theft continuation (pp.358-362).

**Q1 Translation (P05 vs P06).** Lilly uses translation repeatedly as a bare
alternative route beside direct application and house placement: p.304 ("if
none of these happen, yet if there be translation of light between them, then
it shall be effected by the meanes of Friends or Acquaintance"), p.304 route
list, p.305 (agent described by the translator's house), p.328 (fugitive:
"there be any translation of light betwixt them, it sheweth the same with more
facility"), p.358 (theft: "application or translation of light between the
Lord of the 8th, and the Lord of the second"). His working definition on p.317:
"If the Significators aspect not one another, but some Planet transfers their
influence one to another, and this with a benevolent aspect". Nowhere in this
range does he require that the principals had separated from each other (p.317
implies the opposite: translation applies where they have no aspect), nor
reception of the translator by the principal it leaves, nor a lighter
translator, nor (explicitly) no intervening contact. Lunar translation (p.329
fugitives, p.359 thief): Moon separating from one principal and "joyned
immediatly" to the other - Interp.: "immediatly" is the only trace of a
no-intervening-contact condition. Conditions found that are in neither P05 nor
P06: the transfer is "with a benevolent aspect" (p.317); the translator's own
state is checked - not retrograde, combust, unfortunate, cadent, nor in
square/opposition to an infortune without reception, best a Fortune
(pp.317-318); an "ill Planet that carries the light" marks a messenger of the
breach (p.311). p.360 glosses "received any vertue from evill Planets, viz.
separated from them" - separation transfers the left planet's virtue (the
idea behind translation). Interp.: this range supports a structural (P05-like)
reading plus a translator-quality gate; nothing here supports P06's
principal-history or reception-by-house/triplicity/term conditions.

**Q2 Prohibition (B01/B02).** Named, never mechanised: "whether any
frustration or prohibition be before the perfect aspect: the Planet frustrating
describes the party or cause hindering" (p.301, aph. 38); "who frustrates their
aspect, or prohibits them, or interjects his Rayes betwixt the Significators"
(p.305); "no prohibition, frustration or abscission, or Retrogradation of the
principall Significators intervening" (p.317). No statement whether the
interposer touches one principal or both, or must be swifter. "interjects his
Rayes" names aspectual interposition (B02) as a distinct mode. The blocker is
an "evill Planet" (p.305); its house/rulership names the hindering party
(pp.301 aph. 33, 305-306, 311).

**Q3 Refranation/frustration (B03/B04).** Frustration is applied to aspects,
not only conjunction: "frustrates their aspect" (p.305); "The application of
the Significators frustrated, notes the Marriage to be broken off" (p.305);
frustration/abscission blocking a sextile/trine (p.317); "before the perfect
aspect" (p.301). Refranation is not named; "Retrogradation of the principall
Significators intervening" blocks a sextile/trine (p.317) - plural, so
Interp.: either principal, wider than B03's applying planet. Retrograde or
first-station significator is ill generally (p.299 aph. 13); principal
retrograde, cattle die in pound (p.328). Stations elsewhere are descriptive
(pp.329, 360).

**Q4 Heaviness (H01).** p.308-309: "he that is the receiver be an heavy or
ponderous Planet, the querent shall be Master" - absolute ("a heavy planet"),
not comparative. p.309: "The Lord of the seventh more weighty, and in an angle,
she will be master ... if neither ... be in angles, then note the weightier" -
comparative, consulted after angularity. p.310: "the Planet who receiveth the
light of the Significators if he be a heavy Planet" (collector context). p.304:
"collection by a More weighty Planet". No definition anywhere in range; no
passage like "Saturn though a ponderous planet yet being retrograde" found.
p.299 aph. 15 treats "slow in motion" separately (prolongs; hardly performed);
p.300 aph. 27 groups Saturn/Jupiter/Mars against Venus/Mercury. Interp.: the
absolute "a heavy Planet" fits a fixed class/order better than instantaneous
speed, but the text does not decide; only pp.304 and 310-311 are perfection
contexts.

**Q5 Collection (P07).** p.304: "any collection by a More weighty Planet" -
heavier collector; nothing there on reception or non-beholding. pp.310-311: a
heavy planet receiving the light of the significators, if "hindered by
[square] or [opposition] of an ill Planet, or be Cadent, the intended Marriage
shall be broken off againe, though at present it is very feasible" -
Interp.: collector conditions (cadency, malefic hard aspect) absent from P07.
p.318-319: when the 7th lord "behold not the ascendant, but another Planet who
is not impedited, yet beholds the ascendant", reconciliation by a mediator
(unnamed route; querent side is the ascendant, not its lord). "behold ...
though long out, viz. if the aspect be by many degrees distance" (p.332)
suggests a sign-based "behold" in at least one context. Nothing settles
`sign_aversion` vs `no_current_aspect` for collection.

**Q6 Good/proper houses (Q01).** p.317: sextile/trine "out of the ascendant
and eleventh, or ninth and seventh, or seventh and fifth houses ... the Match
will be concluded" - nearest thing to a list for P02 (Interp.: each
significator in its own angle or a house sextile to the 1/7 axis). p.317:
joining "in the ascendant, eleventh or tenth, hardly in the seventh". p.325:
2nd/5th/11th favourable for lost cattle (topic-specific). p.299 aph. 11 uses
"good house" undefined. p.301 aph. 34: angle > succedent > cadent.

**Q7 Reception overriding (P08).** Reception is a route of its own
("Reception of the Significators", p.304), and Lilly writes of joining "with
Reception, with or without any aspect" (p.315). Reception converts hard
aspects: square/opposition with reception "brought to passe with a kind of
slownesse" (p.302); "A [square] aspect with Reception of Significators,
perfects the matter, but with a little difficulty; if no Reception be, there's
onely hopes" (p.317); square/opposition without reception "come to nothing"
(p.317), "never" (p.330); hard aspects need "mutuall Reception, and perfect
aspect" (p.318). Limits: sextile/trine "with or without Reception" still need
"no prohibition, frustration or abscission, or Retrogradation ...
intervening" (p.317) - reception is not said to cancel blockers;
"Notwithstanding Reception, if he be an Infortune, he performes but little"
(p.299 aph. 18); mutual reception after separation leaves "nought but
opportunity" (p.315). Interp.: in this range reception overrides aspect
quality, not blockers; no passage says it overrides prohibition, frustration
or refranation.

**Q8 Conjunction house class / malevolent aspect (P01/B05).** Conjunction
perfects easily "in a Signe agreeing to their owne nature" (p.301 aph. 37);
grouped with square/opposition as "much adoe" in marriage (p.305) but with
sextile/trine for recovering fugitives (p.328). Alkindus (p.303, attributed):
applying planets both cadent, no marriage; in angles, perfected after delay
even by square/opposition. "joyned ... corporally by aspect" (p.313): "joined"
covers aspects. Malevolence: the hinderer is an "evill"/"ill Planet"
(pp.305, 310, 311); two infortunes' good aspect "will ... have no effect"
(p.300 aph. 24). Interp.: leans to the malefic-planet reading; no definition.

**Q9 Route precedence / perfection.** Lilly consults in order - direct
application or house placement, then translation ("if none of these happen",
p.304), then the natural significators Sun/Venus ("if none of these things
be", p.319) - but never says one cancels another. House placement (7th lord in
1st, 1st lord in 7th) is repeatedly a co-equal route (pp.303, 304, 307, 328,
358-359). Direction matters (quesited applying to querent = easier, pp.303,
328). Combustion disables perfection outright (p.300 aph. 23). Timing by
degrees only "when you find strong testimonies ... and that the Significators
are swift" (p.307). Marriage perfection presupposes "freedome of will" (p.317).

**Non-findings.** pp.306 (hinderer catalogue only), 308 (nobility; MS notes by
a later hand ignored), 312-314 (chastity), 316, 320-327 (strays, place,
distance, pound), 331, 361-362: no P4 mechanics beyond the notes below.
OCR pointers: "p.330" translation line is printed p.328; "p.360" line is p.358.

## Passage log

### p.301, doctrine, general aphorisms (Chap. XLVIII, italic, nos. 30-39)
- Aphorism 37: "Planets that are Significators in any thing, if they are in [conj.], and in a Signe agreeing to their owne nature, then the thing quesited after is brought to perfection with much ease and facility, else not."
  Paraphrase: a conjunction of significators in a congenial sign perfects easily.
  Q8 (P01): conjunction perfection qualified by sign nature, not house class. Interp.: an additional qualifier for P01 not in the p.111-113 definitions.
- Aphorism 38: "Have speciall regard to the Significators, and whether any frustration or prohibition be before the perfect aspect: the Planet frustrating describes the party or cause hindering the matter demanded."
  Q2/Q3: states frustration/prohibition operate "before the perfect aspect" (aspect, not just conjunction, is the thing interrupted); the interposer signifies the hindering party. Does not say which principal is touched, nor swiftness. Interp.: "perfect aspect" wording weakly supports frustration applying to aspects as well as conjunctions (B04 generalisation), but it lumps frustration and prohibition together and gives no mechanics.
- Aphorism 34: nearer an angle more good; succedent less; cadent little. Q8: house-class grading of a significator's position (not of the conjunction itself).
- Aphorism 36: infortune aspecting significator, both peregrine/retrograde/cadent → inevitable mischief. Not route-related.

### p.302, Chap. XLIX "Of Marriage" opening, doctrine
- Significators: querent = ascendant, its lord, Moon, planet Moon separates from; quesited = 7th, its lord, planet Moon applies to; Sun (man) / Venus (woman) "partners".
- "behold what application the Lord of the ascendant or [Moon] hath with the Lord of the seventh, and what application that Planet hath from whom the [Moon] is separated, with the Planet to whom she doth apply"
  Interp.: Moon's separation-application sequence treated as a querent-to-quesited linkage (structural translation-like reading, Q1/P05-flavoured), with no conditions stated.
- "if the application be by [square] or [opposition], and with reception, it signifieth that it shall be brought to passe with a kind of slownesse, labour and travell"
  Q7/P08: hard aspect + reception perfects, slowly. Reception mitigates aspect quality; not stated to override absence of aspect or blockers.
  Continues on p.303.

### p.303, doctrine, marriage (cont.) and "Aphorismes of Alkindus touching Marriage"
- Cont. from p.302: if the 7th lord (or planet the Moon applies to) applies to the 1st lord (or planet the Moon separates from), or the 7th lord is in the ascendant, "the matter shall be brought easily to passe ... chiefly if there be an application by [trine] or [sextile] aspect."
  Q8/Q9: direction of application (quesited to querent) = easier; placement of 7th lord in 1st counted as positive testimony alongside aspects (non-aspect route by house position).
- Alkindus (attributed, not Lilly's own): applying and applied planet "both cadent from the angles, and especially if their Lords doe not behold them" → good hopes at first, then "no Marriage at all performed".
  Q8 (P01 house class): cadency of both applying principals denies, for aspects generally, not only conjunction. "their Lords doe not behold them" = dispositors aspecting the significators.
- Alkindus: Moon, Sun, Venus, both lords "in angles, and they beholding one another, or if their Lords behold them, though with [square] or [opposition]" → first despair/suspended, afterwards brought to pass.
  Q8/B05: hard aspects from angles still perfect (with delay). Interp.: angularity can compensate for hard aspect; no reception stated.
- Marginal note: "* Or, if the Lord of the seventh apply to the Lord of the ascendant" (either direction).
- New sub-head "Of Marriage, whether it shall take effect or no?" begins (same significators as p.302).

### p.304, doctrine, "Of Marriage, whether it shall take effect" (cont.) and second "Of Marriage"
- Direction of application: 7th lord in ascendant or applying to 1st lord → "unwillingly be consented unto by the party desired"; 1st lord/Moon applying to 7th lord or in the 7th → querent obtains "by his owne labour". (Contrast p.303, where 7th lord applying = "easily ... with great good will". The two adjacent rules disagree on the quesited's willingness; recorded, not resolved.)
- "but if none of these happen, yet if there be translation of light between them, then it shall be effected by the meanes of Friends or Acquaintance"
  Q1 (P05/P06): translation used as a fallback route when direct application/placement is absent; agent = friends. No conditions stated (no prior separation of principals, no reception, no speed). Interp.: structural use, consistent with P05; says nothing against P06 either.
  Q9 (route precedence): translation is consulted only "if none of these happen" — direct application/house placement ranked first. Interp.: a qualitative order of consultation, not a rule that direct denial erases translation.
- Moon in 10th, Moon applying to Venus, Sun applying to Venus (esp. Sun dignified in 7th): effected by mediation of friends.
- "Of Marriage" (second section): man's significators = 1st lord, Moon, planet Moon separates from, Sun; woman's = 7th lord, planet Moon applies to, planet in 7th, Venus. Woman querent mutatis mutandis.
- Ways "It shall be": (1) 1st lord or Moon in the 7th; (2) planet the Moon separates from applies to planet the Moon applies to; (3) Sun and Venus apply to each other; (4) 1st lord in 7th or 7th lord in 1st; (5) "any translation of light from the Significators, or Reception of the Significators, or any collection by a More weighty Planet, the * Signifiers in interchangeable Dignities, ..." (continues p.305).
  Marginal note to "interchangeable Dignities": "Viz. one in the Termes, the other in the Triplicity of the Significator, or the like."
  Q1: translation again listed bare, as one route among several.
  Q5 (P07): "collection by a More weighty Planet" — collector must be more weighty; nothing on reception of the collector or on non-beholding here.
  Q7 (P08): "Reception of the Significators" listed as an independent route alongside translation and collection (supports P08 as its own route). Marginal note shows mixed reception (term vs triplicity) counts as "interchangeable Dignities", i.e. reception by minor dignities, not only house/exaltation.
  Q4 (H01): "More weighty" not defined here.

### p.305, doctrine, marriage (cont.); "What shall be the occasion of hindring the Marriage"
- Route list (cont.): "... the [Moon] in the seventh giving vertue to the Lord of the ascendant, or Lord of the seventh." (another lunar transfer form).
- "The Significators of the party desired, not beholding the Significators of the Querent, noteth the love of some other more then the Querent" — "not beholding" used as testimony about affection, not as a collection condition. Q5: no sign-vs-aspect clarification.
- "The applicatio[n] of the Significators frustrated, notes the Marriage to be broken off, by such a person or thing as that Signifier noteth, which you may know by the house he is in & Lord of"
  Q3 (B04): frustration of an *application* of significators (not restricted to conjunction in wording); effect = broken off, agent described by the frustrating planet's house/rulership.
- "contrariwise, the Marriage being presaged by translation of light, or collection, it shall be furthered by such a one (as above mentioned)" — agent read from the translator's/collector's house (2nd friend promising dowry, 3rd brother, 10th mother, 5th/11th friend, 6th uncle/aunt/servant).
  Q1: translator described by house lordship/position (P06's "house rulership describes the agent" also usable under P05). No conditions on translation stated.
- "Marriages promised by [conj.] [square] or [opposition], note performance with much adoe; [trine] or [sextile], easie; with Reception, best of all."
  Q7/Q8: conjunction grouped with square/opposition as "much adoe" (!) in this marriage context. Reception = best, but as an intensifier of an existing promise.
- Hindrance: "consider what evill Planet it is who doth hinder the Reception of the disposition of the Significators ... or who frustrates their aspect, or prohibits them, or interjects his Rayes betwixt the Significators"
  Q2/Q3: four named blocking modes: hindering reception; frustrating "their aspect" (frustration explicitly applied to aspects, B04 generalisation); prohibition; interjecting rays (aspectual interposition, B02). The blocker is called an "evill Planet" here. No swiftness or which-principal detail. Agent identified by its house (2nd lord → money on querent's side; continues p.306).

### p.306, doctrine, hindering planet by house (cont.)
- Catalogue: hindering planet as lord of 3rd/4th/5th/6th/8th/9th/10th/11th/12th, or a planet in the 7th, names the source of obstruction. 12th lord: "the matter shall be much retarded ... and will quite break the matter."
  Q2/Q3: only the agent-description of a blocker; no mechanics. Interp.: blocker effect wording here ranges from "retarded" to "quite break", i.e. Lilly does not keep one fixed effect per blocker type in practice (relevant to B01/B02 "hindered and retarded" vs B03 "never").

### p.307, doctrine, "Whether a man shall Marry", "The time of Marriage", "How many Husbands"
- p.306-7: "by the same rules, rightly varied, you shall find who will assist or befriend the querent" — helpers read the same way as hinderers.
- Marriage: Moon to Sun/Venus by good aspect, or 1st lord in 7th / 7th lord in 1st, or good aspect between them. Signifies marriage (house placement again a parallel testimony to aspect).
- Time: degrees of the application (Moon to Sun/Venus, or 1st lord to 7th lord, or reverse), moveable = days, common = months, fixed = years. "This must be understood when you find strong testimonies of Marriage, and that the Significators are swift."
  Q9/T01: timing conversion applies only with strong testimony and swift significators; "swift" undefined. Q4: "swift" used as a quality of a significator, not as comparative heaviness.
- How many husbands: Arabic rules attributed; non-finding for P4.

### p.308, doctrine, "From what part one shall Marry", "What manner of person", "Whether man or woman be more noble", "Who shall be Master of the two" (begins)
- Sun and Moon in square/opposition "note contention, separation and discords" (descriptive, not perfection).
- Nobility: 7th lord angular vs 1st lord succedent → woman better descended; "A more assured way is, by observing whether of the Significators is most superiour, and most potentiall in essentiall Dignities; if no such thing be, who is best placed in an angle". Interp.: "superiour" here probably = elevated/higher placed or of higher rank, not the superior/inferior planet class; ambiguous, not used for H01.
- "Who shall be Master": "if the [Moon] or the Lord of the ascendant be received in an angle, and he that is the [receiver] ..." continues p.309 (the OCR "receiver ... heavy or ponderous" hit).
- Annotator's MS marginalia ("Gadbury saith ...") on pp.302 and 308 are later handwriting, not Lilly; ignored.

### p.309, doctrine, "Who shall be Master" (end), "Whether she be rich", "Legitimate", "How they shall agree", "Disagree"
- p.308-9: "if the [Moon] or the Lord of the ascendant be received in an angle, and he that is the receiver be an heavy or ponderous Planet, the querent shall be Master; and whether Significators shall be found weak, ill dignified, or in cadent houses, that party shall be subject."
  Q4 (H01): "heavy or ponderous" used as a property of a single planet (the receiver), apparently absolute (a class: heavy planets), not a pairwise comparison. No definition given. Interp.: reads most naturally as traditional heaviness (Saturn/Jupiter/Mars, possibly Sun), i.e. `traditional_order` or a superior class, not instantaneous speed; but the page does not say. Not a perfection context (mastery in marriage).
- "Whether the Marriage be Legitimate": significators vitiated or joined to Saturn/Mars (when those are not significators), or with South Node → unlawful marriage. Non-P4.
- "How they shall agree": "If the Figure performe Marriage" — 1st and 7th lords in trine/sextile agree well; "The Lord of the seventh more weighty, and in an angle, she will be master, or strive for it: if neither the Lord of the ascendant, or of the seventh be in angles, then note the weightier, for that party signified by him, shall be master".
  Q4 (H01): "more weighty"/"the weightier" = pairwise comparison between the two lords; angularity is consulted first, weight decides when neither is angular. No definition of weight. Interp.: comparative, so compatible with any H01 reading; contextual pairing with "heavy or ponderous Planet" (same topic, previous paragraph) suggests a fixed planetary order rather than daily speed, but this is inference.
- "Disagree": 1st and 7th lords in square or opposition ... (cont. p.310).

### p.310, doctrine, "Disagree" (end), "Who shall be the cause of their Strife", "That the Marriage shall be broken" (begins)
- "no application between the Planet the [Moon] separates from, and the Planet unto whom she doth apply, notes contention alwayes" — the Moon's separate-from/apply-to pair should itself apply (cf. p.302, p.304 route 2). Non-perfection (concord) context.
- "That the Marriage shall be broken": "Behold the Planet who receiveth the light of the Significators if he be a heavy Planet, and be hindered by [square] or [opposition] of an ill Planet, ..." (cont. p.311).
  Q5/Q1/Q4: "receiveth the light of the Significators" = a collector-like (or translation-receiving) planet; "a heavy Planet" again absolute, as on p.309. Interp.: this is collection language (planet receiving the light of both significators) and the heavy condition matches P07's "collector heavier"; the clause adds that the collector can itself be blocked by a square/opposition of an ill planet (an aspectual hindrance on the collector, not on the principals). "ill Planet" = malefic, i.e. a malefic-planet reading of hindrance here (B05 context).

### p.311, doctrine, "That the Marriage shall be broken" (end); "Whether a Man or his Wife shall dye first"
- Cont.: "... hindered by [square] or [opposition] of an ill Planet, or be Cadent, the intended Marriage shall be broken off againe, though at present it is very feasible."
  Q5 (P07): a heavy receiving/collecting planet that is afflicted by a malefic's square/opposition, or cadent, breaks the matter. Interp.: condition on the collector's own state (house class, malefic hard aspect) not in P07 as defined.
- "If there be an ill Planet that carries the light between the Significators, it shall be by meanes of a Messenger; describe that Planet"
  Q1: translation ("carries the light") by a malefic in the breaking-off context: the translator is the agent of the breach. Interp.: translation by an ill planet transmits a negative outcome; no conditions on separation/reception/speed.
- Ill planet hindering as lord of 2nd/8th = dowry; 3rd = brother; 4th/10th = parent.
- Marginal: Arabic Moon-degree aphorisms "not overmuch to be credited without consent of other Significators".
- Death first: whose significator first goes to combustion; timing by sign quality. Non-P4.

### p.312-313, doctrine, chastity/honesty questions
- p.312: significatrix "in [trine] or [sextile], in Reception, out of any mutuall Dignities" — Lilly uses reception "out of any mutual dignities" (any essential dignity, mutual) as testimony; Q7 minor: reception not restricted to house/exaltation.
- p.313: "If the [Moon] be joyned to [Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun] corporally by aspect, so that there is between them but five degrees or lesse" — note "joyned ... corporally by aspect" within 5 degrees: "joined" covers aspects too. Q8/B01 terminology: Lilly's "joyned" is not restricted to bodily conjunction. Interp.: caution when reading "joined" in rules as conjunction-only.
- Otherwise non-P4 (descriptive judgements).

### p.314, doctrine, "Whether a woman is honest", "Whether a woman trades with any but her Husband"
- "corporally joyned in one Signe ... but if they be separated asunder by three degrees" — bodily conjunction within the same sign; separation after 3 degrees reads as a completed past event. Q8 minor.
- "if they be both joyned to the Lord of the ascendant, whether with Reception or [conj.]" — Moon and 7th lord both applying to the 1st lord. Interp.: structurally a collection-like figure but with the querent's own significator as receiver; not called collection.
- "The Lord of the seventh, the [Moon], or both, separate from any other Planet but the Lord of the ascendant, and he not separate..." (cont. p.315): separation history of the significators matters for the judgement. Q1 weak relevance (attention to which planet a significator has just separated from).

### p.315 (running head misprinted "215"), doctrine, adultery/lover questions (cont.)
- Separation >3 degrees = relationship ended (past); "in one Signe in [conj.]" vs "in one degree" graded.
- "Mars did receive the Moon ... she did a long time love one, but she hath little to doe with him now ... but if there be a mutuall Reception between them, they still love one another ... and there wants nought but opportunity."
  Q7 (P08): mutual reception keeps the relationship alive despite the significators having separated; but "there wants nought but opportunity" — Interp.: mutual reception gives inclination/affection, not by itself the completed act. Weak evidence against P08 as an automatic override.
- "The Lord of the seventh joyned to [Venus] with Reception, with or without any aspect, or else by a [trine] or [sextile], or [square] without Re[ception] ..." (cont. p.316).
  Q7: Lilly explicitly contemplates "Reception, with or without any aspect" as a joining. Direct textual support for reception without aspect counting as a connection (P08 "without aspects").

### p.316, doctrine, lover questions (cont.)
- 7th lord joined to the Sun: "if it be with Reception, he hath or may have, if he please, to doe with her; but if it be without Reception, he cares not for her" — Q7: reception decides whether a joining is effective/willing. Reception as a qualifier on a contact, not a replacement for it.
- Otherwise descriptive (planet in the 7th). No P4 mechanics.

### p.317 (KEY PAGE), doctrine, "If a Marriage shall be perfected or no" (Lilly's own section)
Crop checked: `crops/c/p317_1.jpg` (middle third).
1. "If the Lord of the ascendant or [Moon] be joyned to the Lord of the seventh, in any of the dignities of the Lord of the seventh, and in the ascendant, eleventh or tenth, hardly in the seventh, the querent shall obtaine the party desired."
   Q7: the applying significator located in the quesited-lord's dignities (i.e. received by the 7th lord) plus a contact. Q6 (Q01): the houses named for this joining are 1st, 11th, 10th; the 7th is "hardly".
2. "If both Significators behold each other with [sextile] or [trine], out of the ascendant and eleventh, or ninth and seventh, or seventh and fifth houses, with or without Reception, no prohibition, frustration or abscission, or Retrogradation of the principall Significators intervening, the Match will be concluded if the querent please"
   Q6 (Q01): concrete house pairs from which a sextile/trine perfects: 1-11, 9-7, 7-5. Interp.: these are exactly the sextile pairs bracketing the 1st/7th axis, i.e. "good houses" = the significator's own angle and the houses sextile to it; succedent/cadent houses (11, 9, 5) included. Lilly does not call them "good houses" here, but this is the P02 context (sextile/trine "out of good houses", S4-13), so it is the closest thing to a list.
   Q2/Q3: the blockers named for a sextile/trine are "prohibition, frustration or abscission, or Retrogradation of the principall Significators intervening". So: (a) frustration and abscission are named as blocking an *aspect* (sextile/trine), not only a conjunction (B04 generalisation supported); (b) "Retrogradation of the principall Significators" is listed as a blocker (refranation-type, B03), applying to either principal ("principall Significators", plural) — Interp.: wider than B03's "the applying planet", since the heavier principal's retrogradation is not excluded.
   Q7 (P08): "with or without Reception" — reception is not needed for sextile/trine. Note the blockers are listed *in addition* to "with or without Reception": Interp.: reception does not cancel them in this clause.
   "if the querent please (for we doe suppose a freedome of will in this nature)" — perfection = matter available to querent's will.
3. "if a [square] or [opposition] be between the Significators (and no Reception) the matter will come to nothing."
4. "A [square] aspect with Reception of Significators, perfects the matter, but with a little difficulty; if no Reception be, there's onely hopes, no grounds whereby to judge the thing shall be effected really."
   Q7/Q8: in marriage, square without reception does not perfect; with reception it perfects. "If the Significators aspect not one another, but some Planet transfers their influence one to another, and this with a benevolent aspect, then shall the matter be brought to passe by one signified by that Planet, whose description you may frame according to the Signe wherein he is, and his quality from the house he is Lord of"
   Q1 (P05/P06): translation defined by Lilly as a fallback when "the Significators aspect not one another" (no aspect between principals — not "separated from each other"); condition stated: "with a benevolent aspect" (translator's legs by sextile/trine — Interp.: legs, or at least the transfer, must be by good aspect).
