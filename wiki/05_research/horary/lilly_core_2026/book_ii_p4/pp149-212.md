## Findings by passage

### p.151, doctrine, absent party dead or alive (1st house); Q1
- "See alfo if there be any tranflation of the light of the Lord of the
  Afcendant unto the Lord of the eight ... or ... any tranflation or carrying
  the vertue or influence of the Lord of the eight unto the Lord of the
  Afcendant"
- **Lilly states:** translation in either direction between the Lord of the
  Ascendant and the Lord of the 8th is a testimony of death. The same list has
  bodily conjunction, opposition, and dwelling (Lord of the 8th in the
  Ascendant).
- **Interpretation:** translation is structural (P05-like). Its direction is
  explicitly symmetric. Lilly gives no condition of reception, lightness, prior
  separation or intervening contact.
- p.151 also: "If the Lord of the Afcendant be feperated from a bad Afpect of the
  Lord of the fixt ... lately ficke". A recent separation is read as past events.

### p.153, C02; Q1 (translation), P-routes
- "had the [Moon] feparated from [Jupiter], Significator of the Youth, and
  prefently applyed to a good or indifferent afpect of the [Sun] ... I would
  have adjudged him at his Mafters houfe" (the Sun rules the 10th, the master).
- **Lilly states:** this is a counterfactual. If the Moon had separated from the
  youth's significator and then applied to the master's significator, the youth
  would have been judged to be with the master.
- **Interpretation:** a lunar translation is used as a locator, with a "good or
  indifferent" second leg. No reception, no heaviness, no requirement of the
  principals' history.
- In the actual judgement, Jupiter in the angle of the Ascendant, and the Moon
  applying to a sextile dexter of Saturn (Lord of the 4th), give "two
  teftimonies" that the son is at home.
- Timing: "the 25. of July following ... [Jupiter] and [Venus], being both
  their Significators ... came to a [trine] afpect", and the meeting happened
  that day. A trine perfection is used for timing (P02-type). Lilly mentions no
  reception and no blocker.

### p.155, C02 hypothetical: sudden event
- Venus (Lady of the Ascendant) is in trine with Jupiter, who is in the
  Ascendant, so the outcome is good. "had [Venus] been neerer to the [opposition]
  of [Mars] ... in the fecond ... the Querent would have received fome loffe".
- **Interpretation:** the nearer of two aspects governs. This bears weakly on
  precedence (Q9: nearest applying aspect first). No P4 blockers.

### pp.156-157, C02 hypothetical: absent dead or alive; Q1, Q6, B05
- "Neither is the [Moon] or Lord of the Afcendant joyned to any Planet in the
  eight by [conjunction] but are all free from the malignant beams and afpects
  of the Lords of the 8th or 6th ... Or is there tranflation of light from the
  Lord of the eighth to the Lord of the Afcendant".
- **Lilly states:** "malignant beams" are attributed to the **Lords of the 8th
  and 6th**, that is, to planets by house rulership, not to aspect type.
- **Interpretation (B05):** "malignant/malevolent" can be keyed to the
  significance of the aspecting planet (evil-house lords), a third reading next
  to "malefic planet" and "hard aspect".
- p.157: "[Mercury] Lord of the eleventh, applies to a [square] of [Jupiter] in
  the Afcendant, both of them being in Signes of long afcenfions, which is
  equivalent to a [trine]". News comes in about ten weeks (ten degrees short).
  A square counted as a trine because of the signs of long ascension, used for
  timing news. Relevant to B05 and P03: aspect quality can be modified by sign
  ascension.

### p.157, doctrine, ship (Ch. XXVI); Q7
- After listing the testimonies that the ship is lost: "(unleffe you find
  reception betwixt themfelves) for then the Ship was cafually Shipwrackt, and
  fome of the Sea-men did efcape". With all significators free: "the more fafe
  if any reception be".
- **Interpretation:** reception mitigates a bad outcome but does not reverse it.

### pp.183-184, C05 (end of judgement); Q1, P07
- p.183: "the [Moon] was Lady of the tenth houfe (which fignified his Trade)
  that fhe was transferring the light and nature of [Mercury] and [Mars] to the
  Querent". Lilly advises diligence in his profession.
- **Interpretation:** the Moon is the translator and is herself the 10th lord.
  The translator's own house describes the means (trade). This matches P06's
  "translator's house rulership describes the agent". Lilly states no
  reception or other condition here.
- p.183: Mars is peregrine but Lord of the 2nd and 7th, "the promifing Planet",
  and shows the performance "(but not without fome manner of obftruction;)".
  Debility qualifies the manner of perfection, not whether it happens.
- p.184: "in all Judgments you muft warily confider the nature of the houfe
  wherein the application of your Significators are" (Venus on the 11th cusp,
  so the wealth comes through friends). **Q6/Q9:** the house where the
  application happens colours the outcome.

### pp.184-186, doctrine, "Of that Planet or Planets impediting the effecting
### or performance of what is demanded in every Question" (2nd-house chapter, general)

This is the central text in my range.

1. **Planet impediting (p.184):** if the Lord of the Ascendant, the Moon or the
   quesited's significator "is joyned to an evill Planet, evill difpofed,
   without reception; or ... joyned to an Infortune, and he ill difpofed, and
   receive him not, it prenotes the deftruction of the thing quefited." Joining
   a malefic without reception destroys the matter.
2. **Ill disposed defined (pp.184-185):** "when Peregrine, Retrograde, Combuft,
   Cadent, from the Afcendant or houfe of the thing demanded, fo that he beholds
   not the houfe ... in this nature the afpect to the houfe is better then to
   the Lord thereof; fo any Planet in his Fall or Detriment, may properly be
   called Deftroyer or Obftructor".
3. **Reception rescues (p.185), Q7:** if a significator or the Moon is joined to
   an unfortunate planet (retrograde, combust, cadent), "then obferve if
   Reception intervene; which if there be, it fignifies the perfection of the
   matter, though with wearineffe and much folicitation: If no reception be, the
   matter will come to nothing, though there have been much probability".
4. "If the Planet who receives ... be free from misfortunes, neither receiving or
   received, it perfects the matter with facility." (Reading: the planet
   receiving the significator, and the planet receiving that one, are both free.)
5. **Benevolent intermediary afflicted (p.185):** if the planet the significator
   is joined to is a benefic that is itself in aspect with a malefic, "and he
   impedited and not receiving the former Planet, the matter will not then be
   brought to perfection". Q7/Q2: a second-order chain, where the planet
   receiving the joining matters.
6. **Reception overrides hard contact (p.185), Q7:** "Doe you ftill materially
   confider if the Planets afpects be without reception, for when they are in
   reception, things are brought to paffe, though with fome trouble".
7. **Abscission as rescue; "prohibited" used for joining a malefic (p.185),
   Q2/Q3/Q9:** "ever confidering whether any Planet doe cut off the light and
   vertue of the Significators before their perfect [conjunction] with an evill
   Planet; if fuch a thing happen, it hinders not, but that the matter may be
   perfected and accomplifhed: but if no abfciffion of light intervene, whereby
   the malevolence of the Infortune may be taken off, the matter is prohibited,
   and will not be effected."
   - **Lilly states:** a cutting-off (abscission) by an intervening planet
     *before* a harmful conjunction is perfect *protects* the matter. Without
     it, the matter is "prohibited".
   - **Interpretation:** the interposition mechanism (B01/B02) is symmetric in
     effect. Interposition before a *harmful* perfection is good, and it is
     framed from one significator's side only (it cuts off "the light and
     vertue of the Significators" before *their* conjunction with the evil
     planet). The text does not say the interposer must contact both. "Prohibited"
     here names the *unblocked* harmful conjunction, not the B01 event. A runtime
     should not equate "prohibition" with "denial" without recording which
     contact is harmful.
8. **Reception by square/opposition (pp.185-186), Q7/P03/P04:** "judge if
   Reception doe intervene whether it be not by [square] or [opposition], for
   then if a Planet be evill difpofed, then the reception profiteth nothing; the
   leffe when he that is received is impedited: but if reception be by [sextile]
   or [trine], you may confide the matter will be effected; or if the Planet who
   receives be at that time well difpofed, let the reception be by any manner of
   afpect, the matter is performed, be the afpect [square] or [opposition]; if
   the afpect be [trine] or [sextile], it performes the thing, whether the
   Significator be received or not; but provided, the afpect be not feparated,
   but applying".
   - **Lilly states:** (a) a sextile or trine perfects with or without
     reception, if it is applying; (b) a square or opposition perfects only
     with reception *and* a well-disposed receiver; (c) reception by a hard
     aspect with an ill-disposed planet "profiteth nothing".
   - **Interpretation:** reception is a condition on hard-aspect perfection,
     not an override when the receiver is evilly disposed. For P08 this means
     reception does **not** unconditionally override. Its force depends on the
     receiver's disposition. For P03/P04 it gives a reception condition with
     dignity (well disposed) for squares and oppositions, close to S4-14/S4-15.
     The requirement "not separated, but applying" is explicit.
9. **Translation to an infortune (p.186), Q1:** "If any Planet tranflate the
   light or vertue betwixt one Significator and another, and he to whom the
   light is tranflated be an Infortune, and impedited, the Queftion or matter is
   deftroyed, unleffe the Infortune be againe received."
   - **Lilly states:** a translation is spoiled when the *receiving end* of the
     translation is an impeded infortune, unless that infortune is "againe
     received".
   - **Interpretation:** this is the only translation condition in my range that
     involves reception, and it concerns the **principal receiving the
     translated light**, not the principal the translator leaves (P06's
     wording). There is no mention of prior separation of the principals, of
     lightness, or of an intervening contact. "Received" is not qualified by
     dignity. "againe" may mean "in turn", i.e. the translator receives the
     infortune.
10. **Collection (p.186), P07:** "If the Significator of the Querent, or the
    Moon, and Significator of the thing looked after, be joyned to any Planet
    who collects the light of both Planets, be he an Infortune or infortunate,
    he deftroyes the matter, and permits it not to be accomplifhed, unleffe
    himfelfe receive both the Significators; if he receive one onely of them, it
    matters not, the matter will not be performed."
    - **Lilly states:** for an infortune or afflicted collector, the
      **collector must receive both** significators. One is not enough.
    - **Interpretation:** this is the collector receiving the principals.
      Contrast P07's wording "each principal receives the collector". Here the
      direction is the reverse, stated only for a malefic or afflicted
      collector. Nothing on "do not behold", and nothing on the collector being
      heavier. The Moon can stand in for the querent's significator.
11. **Dwelling / hastening (p.186), P09:** "if the Significator of the thing
    demanded be found in the Afcendant, or haftening to the [conjunction] of the
    Significator of the Querent, it imports the matter ... fhall come to the
    Querent, receptions notwithftanding, the [Moon] and other afpects remaining
    in their proper being." The querent's significator in the house of the
    thing desired shows the querent going to it.
    - **Interpretation:** dwelling of the quesited's significator in the
      Ascendant is accepted as a positive testimony here, "receptions
      notwithstanding" (i.e. reception not needed), provided the Moon and other
      aspects are not contrary. This conflicts with the decision record's P09
      "Lilly rejects dwelling alone". Compare also p.205 below.

### p.188, doctrine, querent and siblings agree (Ch. XXIX); P08, Q6
- Concord if the Lord of the 3rd and the Lord of the Ascendant are "in [sextile]
  or [trine] afpect within the orbs of either Planet, or if they be in mutuall
  reception, or if the Lord of the third caft his [sextile] or [trine] to the
  cufp of the Afcendant". Mutual reception stands alone as a testimony,
  parallel to an aspect (P08-like, for concord, not for perfection of an event).
- "within the orbs of either Planet": the orb is taken as either planet's own
  orb, not the sum or mean. Possibly useful for the aspect-orb policy.
- Saturn, Mars or the South Node in the 3rd, "unleffe in their owne effentiall
  Dignities", is evil, and worse if "Peregrine, Retrograde or Combuft, or in any
  malevolent configuration with any other Planet". Even with apparent unity at
  the time of the question, "it not continue".

### p.189, doctrine, absent brother (3rd); Q7
- "if the malignant Planets behold him with a [square] or [opposition], without
  reception ... great perplexity ... but if they behold him with the aforefaid
  afpects, and be in reception ... he will with eafe evade it". Fortunes by
  sextile/trine without reception, or square/opposition with reception, mean
  good.
- Reception converts a hard aspect from malefics into a recoverable
  testimony. This concerns condition, not perfection.

### pp.190-191, doctrine; Q6 (single reading)
- p.190 names "houfes which are naturally ill (as the fixth, eighth and twelfth
  houfes are)". By complement, the good houses are the rest. **Q6:** these pages
  give no positive list of "good houses".
- p.191: the 11th is the house of a friend (safe). Malefics "caft their
  malevolent beames": here "malevolent" is the quality of the evil planet.

### p.192, doctrine, reports (3rd); Q1
- "if I found the [Moon] in the afcendant, tenth, eleventh or third Houfe,
  feperated by a benevolent afpect from any Planet (be he Lord of what Houfe
  foever) and then applying by [sextile] [trine] or [conjunction] to the Lord of
  the afcendant; I fay, I did finde the report or rumour true". The Moon
  applying by a good aspect to the 7th lord gives the enemies victory. A void
  Moon means the news is vain. If the Moon and Mercury are in square or
  opposition with no favourable sextile or trine to the Ascendant, the news is
  false.
- **Interpretation:** a lunar "translation" from *any* planet to the Lord of the
  Ascendant counts. The first leg need not be a principal. Lilly requires a
  benevolent separation and a good application, and an angular or 3rd/11th Moon.
  This is structural, without reception or heaviness conditions.

### pp.193-194, doctrine, reports (single reading)
- The Lords of the 6th, 8th or 12th in the Ascendant are evil (consistent with
  p.190).
- p.194: the retrogradation of Mercury or of the planet the Moon applies to
  makes ill rumours come to nothing. Lilly does not call this refranation and
  uses it of the *applied-to* planet. B03 non-finding: refranation is not named.

### pp.195-197, doctrine and C06 (single reading)
- p.196: "apply to the Lord of the third, or to any Planet pofited in the
  third". A planet placed in the house can stand for the quesited.
- p.197 (C06): "the [Moon] in regard fhe neither applyed to one fignificator or
  other, had not much to doe in this Queftion". **Q1:** Lilly explicitly
  excludes a Moon that contacts neither principal.

### p.198, C06 "When heare of him"; H01 (key passage)
- "[Venus] is Lady of the third, and [Saturn] is Lord of the Afcendant ...
  as well the Significator of the abfent Brother, who is [Venus], as [Saturn]
  Lord of the Afcendant doe apply to each other by a friendly [trine]; for
  [Saturn] though a ponderous Planet, yet being Retrograde and in more degrees of
  the Signe then [Venus], doth by Retrogradation apply to meet her: a very good
  argument that the Querent fhould heare newes of his Brother very fuddenly".
- **Lilly states:** Saturn remains "a ponderous Planet" even when retrograde.
  Retrogradation makes him an applier (mutual application), and that
  signifies suddenness. The exact time of the trine (Eichstadius, 7 Nov 1645,
  about 5 pm, reduced to London "a little after foure") dates the news. Lilly
  adds that a carrier came at about four.
- **Interpretation for H01:** "ponderous" is a **fixed traditional-order
  attribute** and is not recomputed from instantaneous speed. Lilly separates
  it from the question of **who applies**, which follows actual motion
  (retrograde motion toward the other's degree). This supports
  `traditional_order` for the label "ponderous/heavier" and an
  `instantaneous_motion` test for applying versus separating. A
  reading where speed alone decides heaviness would make the "though ... yet"
  sentence pointless. Also: mutual application (both moving toward each other)
  is recognised and treated as favourable or sudden. Compare p.211.
- P02-type trine perfection used for timing. Reception is not mentioned
  (Venus is entering Capricorn, where Lilly notes she has triplicity and term
  on the same page; that is not stated as reception of Saturn).

### p.199, C06 continued (single reading)
- Return dated from the Moon coming "to the body of [Venus]". "There being an
  amicable afpect betwixt the two Brothers Significators", so concord. No P4
  blockers are invoked.

### p.200, C07 Cambridge report (figure and judgement); Q1, B05
- Figure: 11 Apr 1643, 4h31 PM. Ascendant Libra 4.37 with the North Node on the
  cusp. Venus in Pisces 15 (exalted). Mars in Cancer 1.50 (fall). Saturn in
  Aries 2.7, conjunct the 7th cusp. Jupiter in Aries 28.28 (in the 7th per
  Lilly). Moon in Gemini 12.43, cadent. Centre of the figure: "[Moon] a
  [sextile] [Jupiter] ad [square] [Venus]".
- Text: "I faw the [Moon] feparating from [Jupiter], placed in the feventh and
  transferring his light and vertue to [Venus], which gave me reafon to expect,
  that there would come good to us or our fide from this report" (continues on
  p.201 that the enemies would get no benefit).
- **Lilly states:** this is a translation from Jupiter, **a planet placed in the
  7th, not its lord** (Mars is named "Lord of our Enemies Afcendant, viz. the
  feventh"), to Venus, Lady of the Ascendant. The second leg is a
  **square** (the figure says "ad [square] Venus").
- **Interpretation for Q1:** a structural (P05-like) use. The first "principal"
  is a planet in the house, not the lord. Lilly gives no reception of the Moon
  by Jupiter, no prior separation of the principals (Jupiter and Venus are not
  claimed to have been in aspect), no lightness clause beyond the Moon being
  swiftest, and no check for intervening contact. The square second leg does not
  spoil the translation. It is read as benefit, and the benefit is coloured by
  the benefic translated (Jupiter).
- Other testimonies: moveable angles, Mars on the 10th, Saturn on the 7th, and a
  cadent Moon in Gemini are read as a false report.

### p.201, C07 continued and hypothetical sibling (single reading)
- "[Mars] and [Saturn] in a [square]". The enemies' significators in square
  means division among them.
- Hypothetical sibling: the Moon applying by a square dexter in signs of short
  ascension to Venus, with no ill aspect between Venus and Mars, gives concord.
  A square is softened by sign ascension (compare p.157).

### pp.202-204, doctrine, 4th house: father, lands, treasure (single reading)
- These pages are a **non-finding** for P4. Lilly uses no translation,
  prohibition, refranation, collection or reception-override language.

### p.205, doctrine, buying land/houses (4th); Q1, Q7, P09
- "if the Significators be in any effentiall Dignities upon this their
  application or tranflation of light; or their application be by [conjunction],
  it's then probable they will agree ... with little labour: if the application
  or tranflation of light be by [square] or [opposition], the two Parties will
  at laft bargaine, but with many words and probabilities of breaking off, and
  after much expence of time."
  - **Q1:** a translation can be carried by a square or opposition and still
    perfects (with difficulty). The quality condition is the **significators'
    essential dignity**, not the translator's reception.
- "whether onely the Lord of the fourth apply to the Lord of the afcendant, and
  he receive him in any of his Dignities, or if the Lord of the afcendant be in
  the fourth, or the [Moon], or the Lord of the fourth in the afcendant, then
  fhall the Party enquiring buy".
  - Reception "in any of his Dignities" is attached to a one-way application
    (the receiving principal receives the applying one).
- "But if this dwelling in houfes be not, yet if the [Moon] transferre the
  vertue or light of the Lord of the fourth to the Lord of the afcendant, the
  Bargaine will be concluded, but rather by Meffengers or Brokers, then by the
  perfonall treaty of the two principall Agents."
  - **P09:** for purchase questions Lilly *names* "dwelling in houses" and
    accepts it as a route. Lunar translation is the fallback, and it implies
    intermediaries (P05/P06 agent wording).
- "If there be no application or tranflation, or transferring the light of one
  Planet to another, it's not like there will be any Bargaine concluded."
  - Q9: the enumerated routes here are application and translation (and
    dwelling). Collection is not mentioned.

### pp.206-209, doctrine, land quality, leases, removal (single reading)
- A retrograde infortune in the Ascendant means tenants run away. Retrogradation
  is used as "going back". p.209: "an unfortunate Planet in the tenth, or behold
  that houfe with an [opposition] or [square]" means no house will be taken.
  No routing doctrine; **non-finding** for P4 otherwise.

### p.210, doctrine, father's estate (Ch. XXXIV); P08, B05
- "if ... you find the Lord of the fecond and Lord of the fifth in reception,
  the Lord of the fifth being in the fecond, and the Lord of the fecond in the
  fifth, there's no doubt to be made but the Querent fhall have a competent
  fortune out of the Eftate of his Father".
  - **P08:** this is mutual reception **by house, combined with each being in
    the other's house (dwelling)**, with no aspect mentioned and an unqualified
    yes. The same page qualifies it: "but if ... the Lord of the fifth houfe be
    Retrograde, or in fome bad afpect of any malevolent Planet, then fome part of
    that Eftate ... will be wafted". Affliction reduces the amount but does not
    cancel the outcome.
- B05: "bad afpect of any malevolent Planet" and impediting "by [square] or
  [opposition], or ... the [Sun] by Combuftion". Here "malevolent" qualifies
  the planet, and badness is located in the square or opposition.
- Timing a reconciliation: "when that Planet who impedites, and the Lord of the
  afcendant, are approaching to a [sextile] [trine] or [conjunction], and that
  day ... the [Moon] feparating from the one, and applying to the other".
  A lunar translation day is used as an electional marker.

### p.211, doctrine, father's estate continued; H01, Q1, P08
- "If you find the Lord of the fecond and of the fifth, applying by
  Retrogradation to any good afpect, the Querent will receive fome Eftate from
  his Father fuddenly, ere he be aware". Retrograde application is valid
  and means suddenness (as on p.198). It is not treated as refranation.
- "If the [Moon] feparate from the Lord of the fifth, and either have prefently
  after a [sextile] or [trine] to the Lord of the fecond, or of the afcendant, it
  fhewes ftrong and affured hopes". A lunar translation with good aspects, and
  "presently after" implies no gap.
- "if there be any reception betwixt them, viz. the Significators of Brethren
  and Sifters, or tranflation of light, and none betwixt the Lord of the
  afcendant and Lord of the fourth ... the Fathers affection ftands more to
  another ... fo doe the moft powerfull reception of Significators".
  - Reception and translation are parallel bonds between significators.
    Receptions are ranked by power (Q7/Q9).
- An infortune in the 4th without dignity means waiting until it is "tranfited
  out of that Signe", or until it is "Direct, fwift in Motion, Oriental, and in
  [sextile] or [trine] with [Jupiter] or [Venus]". Direct and swift count as
  strengths.

### p.212, doctrine, removal (Ch. XXXV); Q6 (single reading)
- Lilly's own observation: if the Lord of the Ascendant "did lately feparate
  from the [square] or [opposition] of the Lord of the fixth, eighth or twelfth",
  or the Moon "did feparate from any evill afpect of the Infortunes, they being
  Lords of either the feventh or fourth ... and not Friends or Significators in
  the perfon of the Querent", he advised removal.
- The 6th, 8th and 12th are evil houses again. "evill afpect" is the square or
  opposition.

## Non-findings (explicit)

- pp.149-150 (marks and moles, 1st house) and p.158 (parts of a ship): nothing
  bearing on P4.
- pp.202-204 and 206-209: no route or blocker doctrine.
- Across the whole assigned range:
  - **Refranation** and **frustration** are never named.
  - **Prohibition** is used once (p.185), for an unblocked conjunction with an
    evil planet, and not in the B01 sense.
  - There is **no definition or list of "good/friendly/proper houses"**. Only
    the naturally ill houses (6/8/12, p.190; also pp.193, 212) are listed.
  - There is **no use of "conjunction house class"** (angle/succedent/cadent
    perfection speed) beyond C02's angular-means-at-home logic (pp.153-154:
    angle = at home, succedent = not far).
  - "Collection" appears only on p.186. Lilly never discusses "do not behold" or
    a heavier collector in this range.

## Answers to the brief's questions (from this range)

1. **Translation (P05/P06).** Every in-case use is structural: C02 counterfactual
   p.153, C05 p.183, C07 p.200, and the doctrine on pp.151, 156, 192, 205, 210,
   211. None mentions prior separation of the principals, reception of the
   translator by the principal it leaves, or a no-intervening-contact
   condition. A translation can run in either direction (p.151) and its second
   leg can be a square or opposition (p.200 figure, p.205). In C07 it starts
   from a planet *placed in* the house. On p.192 it starts from any planet. A
   Moon that applies to neither significator is excluded (p.197). The only
   reception condition is on the **receiving** end: an impeded infortune that
   receives the translated light destroys the matter unless "againe received"
   (p.186). The translator's own rulership describes the agent (p.183: Moon,
   Lady of the 10th, means trade; p.205: lunar translation means
   brokers/messengers).
2. **Prohibition (B01/B02).** No B01/B02 example in range. p.185 gives the
   mirror case: an intervening abscission "before their perfect conjunction with
   an evill Planet" *protects* the matter. It is framed from the significators'
   side only, with no swifter condition and no requirement that it touch both.
3. **Refranation/frustration.** Not named. Retrograde application is valid
   and sudden (pp.198, 211). Retrogradation of the planet applied to makes ill
   rumours vain (p.194).
4. **Heaviness (H01).** On p.198 Saturn is "a ponderous Planet" even though he
   is retrograde and is the one applying. Ponderousness is a fixed (traditional)
   attribute. Application direction follows motion. No speed-based heaviness
   appears anywhere in range.
5. **Collection (P07).** On p.186 the collector (if infortune or infortunate)
   must **itself receive both** significators, and one is not enough. The
   direction is collector to principals. "do not behold" and heavier collector
   are not mentioned.
6. **Good houses (Q01).** No positive definition. 6/8/12 are "naturally ill"
   (p.190). The house where the application falls colours the result (p.184).
7. **Reception overriding (P08).** Conditional. Reception lets a hard aspect or
   a contact with an afflicted planet perfect "with trouble/wearinesse"
   (p.185). By square or opposition it "profiteth nothing" if the planet is
   evilly disposed, and works only if the receiver is well disposed
   (pp.185-186). Sextile and trine perfect without reception if applying
   (p.186). Mutual reception alone gives "no doubt" (p.210, 2nd/5th lords in
   each other's houses), reduced but not cancelled by retrogradation or a bad
   aspect. Mutual reception counts as a concord testimony parallel to an aspect
   (p.188). Reception mitigates shipwreck (p.157). Lilly never says reception
   overrides prohibition or refranation.
8. **P01/B05.** "Malevolent/malignant" is attached to planets (malefics, p.191,
   p.210), to the Lords of evil houses (6th/8th, p.156), and to the square and
   opposition as "evill" aspects (pp.185, 210, 212), so all three readings occur.
   Hard aspects can be softened to trine-equivalents by signs of long or short
   ascension (pp.157, 201). Lilly says nothing on conjunction speed by house class.
9. **Other.**
   - The nearest aspect governs (p.155).
   - Debility qualifies the *manner* of perfection, not whether it happens
     (p.183, Mars peregrine "not without fome manner of obftruction").
   - Dwelling in houses is accepted as a positive route for purchase (p.205) and
     for the thing coming to the querent "receptions notwithftanding" (p.186).
     This bears on the P09 rejection, which may come from a different chapter.
   - Applying, not separated, is required for sextile/trine perfection (p.186).
   - Orbs: "within the orbs of either Planet" (p.188).
