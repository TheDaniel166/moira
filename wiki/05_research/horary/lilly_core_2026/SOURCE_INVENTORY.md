## Governing edition and witnesses

**S01: governing witness.** William Lilly, *Christian Astrology, Modestly
Treated of in Three Books*, first edition, London: Thomas Brudenell for John
Partridge and Humphrey Blunden, 1647. The
[Wellcome catalogue](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/s7z7c46y) identifies
the publication, Wing L2215, English language and Public Domain Mark. Its
copy note says the last leaf is wanting; this does not establish a lacuna in
the selected general-rule pages. The
[Wellcome/Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/b30338724) and
[IIIF manifest](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/presentation/b30338724)
provide the photographed witness. Credit: Wellcome Collection, Public Domain
Mark. The manifest has 894 canvases.

**S02: alternate scan witness, same edition.** The
[EEBO-derived Internet Archive item](https://archive.org/details/bim_early-english-books-1641-1700_christian-astrology-_lilly-william_1647)
provides an alternate page image and OCR lineage. Its metadata, OCR and
scandata were acquired. This packet does not claim visual line-by-line
collation of that entire witness or establish whether all its photography
derives from a different physical copy. The title's edition and its scan
lineage must not be confused with independent doctrinal authority.

**S03: modern navigation aid.** The
[Skyscript Christian Astrology index](https://www.skyscript.co.uk/CA/) helps
locate chapters and examples. Modern transcriptions, redrawn charts and
editorial numbering do not override the photographs. No modern chart artwork,
long transcription or copyrighted editorial catalogue is copied into this
packet. The catalogue below uses our own compact descriptions and original
page checks.

**S04: existing Moira admission evidence.** The
[August source dossier](../TRACK_B_HORARY_SOURCE_AND_AMBIGUITY_DOSSIER_2026-08.md)
records the bounded v1 lineage `lilly_1647_ca_books_i_ii_v1`. The
[July perfection validation record](../../../03_validation/WESTERN_ELECTIONAL_PHASE6_LILLY_PERFECTION_VALIDATION_2026-07-15.md)
is historical evidence. Neither replaces the primary pages or current code.

A filename search of `C:\dev\ASTROLOGY-BOOKS-DATABASE` for Lilly/Christian
names did not locate a first-edition source file. That was a filename search,
not a full-text exclusion of unnamed or bundled material. The reproducible

## Page locator and acquisition policy

On 2026-10-22 UTC, acquisition retrieved eight source/metadata files and 79
selected Wellcome JPEG pages without reported acquisition errors. Each image
record contains printed page, exact manifest canvas ID, zero-based canvas
index, requested image URL, byte count and SHA-256. Canvas IDs were resolved
from manifest labels, not inferred from a single offset. Original page/image
indexes differ across the Wellcome and EEBO presentations.

For example, printed p.104 is canvas index 137; p.127 is 160; p.128 is 161;
p.442 is 475; p.491 is 524 in the acquired Wellcome manifest. These are
verified individual mappings, not a rule to derive every other page. A stable
source locator should retain both the printed page and exact canvas/image URL.

Scratch originals and receipts remain outside the repository at
`C:\dev\outputs\lilly-core-admission-20261022\`. The acquisition receipt lists
eight files under `sources/`; `page_acquisition.json` lists the 79 images under
`pages/`. SHA-256 values identify the bytes inspected, not the correctness of
their content. A changed remote image requires a new receipt rather than
silent replacement of the recorded hash.

The complete scan PDF was not downloaded. Selected IIIF page images supplied
the visual checks. OCR is especially unreliable for planetary/zodiac glyphs,
retrograde marks, dates, table columns and numbers near the binding.

## Observed source and documentation conflicts

- P.107 prints two orb sets and allows alternatives. A current fixed table is
  an operational choice, not a uniquely prescribed table in this passage.
- P.113 appears to give a combustion example with Sun 18 Aries and Jupiter
  28 Aries, although the preceding definition uses 8 degrees 30 minutes.
  Retain the apparent inconsistency for alternate-image collation (A13).
- P.397's fish example has a chart/narrative year discrepancy (A24). Calendar
  or year-style explanations are hypotheses until a conversion is documented.
- P.399 contains the Waller/Hopton diagram although navigation references can
  point to p.400. The absent brother's figure is p.196, with judgement at p.197.
- P.455 supplies the figure reused by the separate office question at p.456.
  Do not invent a second independent numerical chart.
- The old July validation prose names Dorothean/Egyptian defaults. Current
  `LillyPerfectionPolicy` explicitly names Lilly's 1647 term/triplicity tables.
  The old prose was not rewritten; this packet records the drift against the
  stated code baseline.

## Reuse and evidence limits

The original 1647 work is the public-domain source. The packet uses compact
paraphrases and factual catalogue entries with original-scan locators. Modern
editorial additions retain their own rights. File availability is not source
admission, and scan agreement is not independent confirmation of an event or
the predictive validity of horary.
