## Shared numerical and transport contract

Supplied evidence is conditional. Dated and window operations borrow the
serving reader, bind its clocks/frames/resources, meter all work, and request
only the selected dependency closure. The full existing MC marriage assembly
keeps its operation order and public vessels. No approximate epoch bins,
cross-request ambient caches or newly inferred source equivalences are used.

arithmetic model. Stable interiors are distinct from uncertain root bands.
Unavailable Lagna, horizon, calendar or historical evidence remains visible;
sampling cannot establish constancy. Bounds apply to parent history, roots,
ephemeris work, retained cells and output bytes. A limit failure never returns
a truncated supposedly complete result. HTTP serializers project the canonical
engine result; they do not contain another astrology evaluator.
