# Pancha Pakshi Uromarisi Backend Standard and Validation Codex

**Status:** Constitutional Phase 11 architecture freeze
**Scope:** Uromarisi-attributed historical activity semantics, Phases 1-10
**Admission:** Private `research_only` data and computation
**Public-data default:** Forbidden

## 1. Governing Object

The subsystem governs one bounded historical-text corpus of activity-position
records. Its primary identity is `(activity, ordinal, verse)`. The corpus is
not a clock, a birth identity system, a relationship table, a condition score,
or a prognostic engine.

The source authority is the hash-bound Uromarisi research fixture chain. It
governs only the bounded transcription and editorial decisions named in those
fixtures. It does not establish a universal Pancha Pakshi canon, medical truth,
or cross-witness equivalence.

## 2. Constitutional Layers

The executable layer order is fixed:

1. source atoms;
2. typed cell classification;
3. classification inspectability;
4. explicit activity-and-ordinal policy;
5. relation-clause formalization;
6. relation inspectability;
7. integrated local-condition profiles;
8. structural aggregate intelligence;
9. structural network intelligence; and
10. full-subsystem hardening.

A later layer may project only facts already explicit in an earlier layer. It
may not use its abstraction to invent earlier source or doctrinal truth.

## 3. Stable Identity and Ordering

The closed materialized corpus contains 24 unique cells in canonical activity
order: EAT, WALK, RULE, SLEEP, DIE, preserving source ordinal order within each
activity. SLEEP ordinal 5 is absent. Verse 250 is an explicit identity conflict
and remains outside every materialized corpus.

Every classification, relation record, local profile, network node, and
hardening receipt must preserve the same activity, ordinal, verse, source
decision identity, and source digest. Containers are immutable tuples. Missing
lookups return `None`; they never select a neighbor or synthesize a record.

## 4. Policy Doctrine

The sole Uromarisi classification policy is
`moira_explicit_uromarisi_activity_ordinal_lookup_research_v1`. Activity and
ordinal are explicit caller inputs. There is no default and no admitted
temporal selector binding.

The separately recorded Bogamuni weighted-Sookshma and equal-fifths selector
policies are not Uromarisi source truth. They cannot be attached to this corpus
without a new, explicit cross-witness composition decision.

## 5. Relation Doctrine

Seventeen cells contain a detected relation clause and seven explicitly do not
record one. Detection is not semantic admission. Ten detected clauses remain
`unresolved_clause`; seven retain only their exact named surface category.

No bounded record establishes a relation endpoint or direction. Consequently:

- admitted edges are empty;
- scored edges are empty;
- topology is not materialized;
- degree, components, paths, and centrality are not evaluable; and
- no missing graph quantity may be represented as numeric zero.

Surface words may not be normalized into friendship, enmity, direction,
reciprocity, weights, loops, or endpoints.

## 6. Condition and Aggregate Doctrine

Each local condition is the exact structural join of one classification and
one relation record under the explicit policy. All 24 profiles have status
`not_evaluable_no_admitted_condition_doctrine`. Favorability is not assigned,
condition score is `None`, prognosis is not performed, and medical use is
forbidden.

The Phase 8 aggregate contains structural counts only: 24 profiles; activity
counts 5, 5, 5, 4, 5; 17 detected and 7 not-recorded relation clauses; 10
unresolved and 7 named-surface clauses; zero admitted and zero scored
relations; and blocked verse tuple `(250,)`. Counts are not ranks, weights,
judgments, or evidence of effect.

## 7. Network Doctrine

Phase 9 projects all 24 profiles into canonical nodes. Its 17 relation
candidates are annotations owned by the node whose source record contains the
detected clause. A candidate is not an edge.

The node identifier format is `<activity>:<ordinal>:verse:<verse>`. Node and
candidate order follows the earlier canonical corpus. Candidate source binding
is identical to its owning profile and relation record. Endpoint inference,
direction inference, duplicate-edge collapse, self-loop repair, and conflict
repair are forbidden.

## 8. Hardening Doctrine

Phase 10 freezes a deterministic structural fingerprint over policy identity,
node identities, source bindings, classifications, semantic markers,
uncertainty counts, relation surface state, evaluation state, candidate
ownership, empty edge sets, blocked conflicts, and metric availability.

The canonical Phase 10 structural SHA-256 is
`2133ad1c72ea5209facbb83ff8f40cfd09c1efea5340df7943fe08ff599cface`.
Its value is regression evidence for this exact bounded structure, not an
external authority validation.

## 9. Failure Doctrine

The subsystem fails closed. It rejects:

- wrong vessel types or mutable/non-tuple containers;
- reordered, missing, duplicated, or foreign members;
- mismatched identities, policy IDs, source decision IDs, or source digests;
- repaired SLEEP ordinal 5 or verse 250;
- injected admitted or scored relations or edges;
- endpoint, direction, condition, score, prognosis, or selector invention; and
- non-hexadecimal, uppercase, truncated, or otherwise invalid SHA-256 values.

No fallback, implicit policy, ambient state, network download, or kernel is
part of this research path.

## 10. Public and Private Boundaries

The modules `_pancha_pakshi_classification`, `_pancha_pakshi_relations`,
`_pancha_pakshi_condition`, `_pancha_pakshi_aggregate`,
`_pancha_pakshi_network`, and `_pancha_pakshi_hardening` are private. Their
historical records, profiles, aggregates, networks, fixtures, and materializers
are not package exports, facade products, manifest capabilities, or REST
contracts.

The only Phase 12-eligible public product is immutable constitutional status:
that Phases 1-12 are complete, the Uromarisi data remains `research_only`, the
network remains private and non-evaluative, relation semantics and topology
are not admitted, medical use is forbidden, and no research data is returned.
This governance product may be exposed at package and facade level. It must not
be represented as a runtime profile or REST computation.

## 11. Validation Codex

Validation uses the project `.venv`, strict known-issue expiry, test mode, and
no network. The evidence classes are:

- **fixture-chain integrity:** canonical newline-normalized SHA-256 links each
  constitutional decision to the prior boundary;
- **regression evidence:** exact cell, count, identifier, status, and Phase 10
  structural fingerprint checks;
- **cross-layer invariants:** identity, source binding, order, policy, absence,
  conflict, and subset relations agree from classification through network;
- **adversarial misuse resistance:** forged bindings, reordering, duplication,
  edge injection, scoring, repair, and mutation attempts fail closed; and
- **public-boundary tests:** private symbols and data remain absent from root,
  facade, Vedic, manifest, and REST surfaces.

This subsystem has no external numerical-oracle claim. It performs no
astronomical calculation and exercises no native path or planetary kernel.
Fixture hashes prove bounded repository identity, not doctrinal truth outside
the named witnesses.

## 12. Constitutional Nonclaims

This standard does not claim translation authority, independent textual
lineage, universal doctrine, temporal-selector attribution, relationship
meaning, condition evaluation, favorability, scoring, prediction, diagnosis,
medical validity, or `corroborated_public` admission. Commissionerate A5 is not
required for this private closure or its governance-status surface; it remains
relevant only to a future stronger corroboration claim.
