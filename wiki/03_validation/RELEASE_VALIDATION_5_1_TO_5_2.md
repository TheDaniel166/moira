# Moira 5.1-5.2 Release Validation Ledger

**Engine baseline:** 5.2.3
**Last verified:** 2026-07-25
**Scope:** public evidence added or materially changed after 5.0.0

This ledger makes recent release evidence discoverable without widening any
claim beyond its named oracle, corpus, invariant, or runtime boundary. It
supplements the three standing validation reports and the versioned release
notes; it does not replace either.

## Status vocabulary

- **Validated** — checked against a named external authority or a clearly
  bounded authority corpus under declared semantics.
- **Partial** — meaningful external or invariant evidence exists, but the
  entire surrounding subsystem is not closed.
- **Documented** — behavior and failure policy are exercised and recorded;
  no broader external-truth claim is made.
- **Research-only** — evidence remains outside the admitted public runtime
  doctrine.
- **Not evaluable** — the available source cannot answer the claimed question
  under matching semantics.
