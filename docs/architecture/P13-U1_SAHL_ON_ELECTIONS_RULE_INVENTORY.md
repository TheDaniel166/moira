**What Sahl *On Elections* is.** A medieval Arabic electional treatise
organized as:

1. **Rootedness layer** (§§1–9): elections are weak without a natal (or
   substitute question) root; group elections diverge by individual nativity.
2. **Sign-nature / quadruplicity / conformity layer** (§§10–20): movable /
   fixed / common signs, straight vs. crooked ascension, elemental and
   special-sign conformity.
3. **Matter-significator + universal fortify layer** (§21, §22a, §28): planet
   of the matter; Ascendant + its lord + Moon + lord of the matter; Dorothean
   remedy when the Moon cannot be cleansed.
4. **Ten Moon impediments** (§22) + general advice (§§23–27).
5. **House-by-house matter inventory** (§§29–143): 2nd through 12th, plus
   letter (§143) outside the twelve signs.

**Election vs. horary / mixed.** Sahl repeatedly uses question charts as
*substitute roots* (§§3–5, §9, §§101–103) and includes pursuit-of-fugitive
material (§142) that is interrogation-shaped. Those are flagged
**[HORARY / MIXED]** where they cannot serve as pure electional gates.
Historical slavery content (§§71–77) is flagged and retained only for
mechanic completeness.

## 1. Rootedness (§§1–9)

This is Sahl’s governing precondition: **an election without a sound root is
weak or worthless**, especially for non-kings.

### 1.1 Elections are weak except for kings (§1)

All agree elections are weak **except for kings**, because kings have a
nativity-root that can strengthen even a weak planet in the course. Even a
bad election can put weak natal planets of a strong nativity to use.

### 1.2 Low-class / merchants / followers (§2)

Do **not** elect for the low-class, merchants, and those who follow in social
status, **unless** the election is based on:

- their **nativities**,
- the **revolutions of those years**, and
- the **nativities of their children**.

### 1.3 Unknown nativity → question as substitute root (§§3a–5c)

| Condition | Rule | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Nativity unknown | Take a **question**; know the effecting of the matter from it; **afterwards** elect according to that (§3a) | Question = second-best root |
| Querent asks about himself | The good/evil has already arrived from his own nativity in the hour he comes (§3b) | Horary-as-root framing |
| Question shows matter will not come to be, or querent/warrior will die | **Beware** — do not proceed to election (§3c) | Hard stop |
| Root of nativity **or** question signifies something horrible | Do not elect (§5a) | |
| Coerced election despite bad root | Even putting all fortunes in stakes and making malefics cadent **will not profit**, esp. low/middle-class (§5b–c) | Risk of electing an Ascendant inimical to the root |

> **Engine note.** Rootedness is doctrine-level: the electional engine must
> eventually accept optional **natal** and/or **question-chart** input. Without
> it, Sahl’s §§1–9 cannot be scored honestly.

### 1.4 Group elections diverge by nativity (§§6–8)

One election for a group (sailors, travelers departing in one hour) yields
different outcomes because of **nativities and distributions** in those years
(§6). People rejoice on bad days and quarrel on good ones (§7). A significator
joined to a malefic by square/opposition/same-sign may still discover good if
that malefic is agreeable as **first lord of the Ascendant**, **lord of its
distribution**, or **lord of the Ascendant of the year-revolution** (§8).

### 1.5 Worthier elections (§9)

Election based on the **Ascendant of a known question or nativity**, or on
the **lord of the Ascendant** (sign of the **profection of the year**), is
more worthy — you know which stars accord with it. “Let your work be like
your election.”

## 2. Sign Nature / Quadruplicities / Straight–Crooked / Conformity (§§10–20)

### 2.1 Cosmic concordance (§§10–11)

Superior and inferior essences combine by concord and are destroyed by
adversity (§11a). Fortunes are temperate; malefics are of harmful nature and
wish to impede. Even if **received**, the malefic essence is not wholly
absent (§11b) — like thieves / citizens of evils.

### 2.2 Movable (cardinal) signs (§§12–13)

| Effect | Detail |
|---|---|
| Tempo | Mobility of matters, quickly; nothing lasting; time not prolonged (§12a) |
| Good for | Sowing seed; buy/sell; betrothal; infirm freed quickly; contention not prolonged; fugitive returns quickly; foreign travel useful (§12a–b) |
| Bad for | Promises (will not move forward / not kept); pronouncements, dreams, rumors false; doctor should not cure; planting; foundations (§12b) |
| Stability rule | Everything begun whose stability is wanted will **not** be stable; begin **unstable / hurried** works under them (§12c) |
| Internal ranking | **Aries & Cancer** faster (more crookedness/mobility); **Libra & Capricorn** stronger and more balanced (§13) |

> **Engine note (§13).** Ranking among movable signs depends on **ascensional
> times** at latitude (crooked = short ascension, straight = long). Do not
> hardcode northern-hemisphere lists as absolute — compute from oblique
> ascension (same doctrine as Dorotheus V.2).

### 2.3 Fixed signs (§§14–15)

| Effect | Detail |
|---|---|
| Tempo | Stability and prolongation; lasting work (§14a) |
| Good for | Building; wedding celebration **after** engagement in movable signs; contracts/claims; foundations (§14a–c) |
| Divorce | Woman divorced in fixed signs will **not** return (§14b) |
| Caution | Judgments/inceptions lack confidence unless fortunes’ testimonies are multiplied (§14b) |
| Imprisonment / anger | Conquered → imprisonment prolonged; angry person not quickly appeased (§14c) ⚠ Crofts reverses anger reading |
| Internal ranking | Scorpio lighter; Leo more fixed; Aquarius slower/worse; Taurus more even (§15) — Dykes notes domicile-rulership overlay on equal ascensional pairs |

### 2.4 Common (mutable / bicorporeal) signs (§§16–17)

| Effect | Detail |
|---|---|
| Good for | Partnerships, brotherhood; work often **repeated**; alteration/resettling; washing head/beard; purifying gold/silver; sending boys to letters (§16a, §17) |
| Bad for | Buy; celebrate wedding (trickery/deception); ship travel (change ship to ship); promises dissolved; infirm healed then **relapses** (§16a–c) |
| Doubling | All good and evil **doubled**; if someone dies, another near him dies in that place (§17) |
| Prison / fugitive / judge | Imprisoned not fixed (except Pisces ⚠ Latin/Arabic); who leaves prison returns; fugitive flees again; judge gives no settled opinion (§16b) |
| Pisces exception | Imprisonment more fixed in Pisces “on account of the rarity of its appearance” (§16b) — ascensional-time rationale |

### 2.5 General placement advice (§18)

- Put **Moon and Ascendant** in houses agreeing with the desired matter.
- Conjoin Moon with **fortunes receiving her** in that sign.
- **Diurnal signs stronger by day**; make Ascendant diurnal and put Moon in
  diurnal signs (Crofts addition; cf. Dorotheus V.5 nocturnal counterpart).

### 2.6 Conformity of special sign classes (§19)

| Sign class | Conformity |
|---|---|
| Airy ⚠ (Crofts “watery”; Dykes/al-Rijāl: airy) | Hunting by land and sea |
| Royal | Kings |
| Signs with voices | Pipers, songs, lute-playing |
| Fiery | Everything with fire |
| Signs of equality (Aries, Libra) | Truth, speaking truthfully, work with scales |
| Changeable / tropical (Cancer, Capricorn) | Change; alteration from thing to thing |

### 2.7 Nature-of-the-work rule (§20)

For every work: consider the **nature of that sign from the orbs** (circles of
heaven); conjoin Moon and lord of Ascendant with that essence; the root of
that nature and its virtue is in the hour of undertaking.

## 3. Matter Significators + Universal Fortify (§21, §22a, §28)

### 3.1 Planetary significators of matters (§21)

| Matter / persons | Work through |
|---|---|
| Lords, princes, great men, city governors, visible people, masters of fights and wealth ⚠ (Crofts: executioners/amnesty) | **Sun** (§21a) |
| Lofty people | **Jupiter** (§21b) |
| Farmers, lowest people | **Saturn** (§21b) |
| Generals, masters of fights | **Mars** (§21b) |
| Women | **Venus** (§21b) |
| Purchases, sales, contentions, writing, businessmen | **Mercury** (§21c) |
| Commingling with queens; inquiry among women | **Moon** (§21c) |

### 3.2 Universal fortify (§22a)

If you wish to begin some work: **adapt the Ascendant and its lord, and the
Moon and the lord of the matter.** Beware the impediment of the Moon, as
Dorotheus and the sages said — ten ways (§22).

> Dykes note: also make the **lord of the Moon** fit (al-Rijāl VII.3.4; cf.
> Carmen V.6.21).

### 3.3 Dorothean remedy when Moon is impeded (§28)

If the Moon is impeded and the matter **cannot be put off**:

- Do **not** give the Moon a role in the Ascendant;
- Make her **cadent from the Ascendant**;
- Put a **fortune in the Ascendant**;
- Strengthen the Ascendant and its lord.

(Attributed to Dorotheus; cf. Carmen V.6.15 / Crofts on V.5.10–11.)

### 4.2 General advice (§§23–27)

**§23 — Waxing Moon in Ascendant; house-aspect doctrine**

- Do not put the Moon **waxing in any Ascendant** — feared for bodily
  infirmities of the election’s owner — **unless** the lord of the Ascendant
  or a fortune aspects the ascending [degree/sign] (§23a).
- A planet not aspecting its own house is like a man absent from home (§23b);
  one that aspects is like the owner who guards it (§23c).

**§24 — Malefic lord of Ascendant; stakes caution**

- If lord of Ascendant is a malefic, make him aspect from **trine or sextile**
  (§24).
- Beware putting lord of Ascendant or Moon (if malefics aspect the Moon) in a
  stake / in the stakes of the Ascendant (§24). ⚠ Latin/Arabic wording
  unclear (Crofts vs Dykes).

**§25 — Lot of Fortune**

- In all beginnings or questions: do not let Lot of Fortune be **cadent from
  the aspect or conjunction of the Moon** (§25a).
- Do not care if Lot is cadent from Ascendant **if** Lot aspects Ascendant and
  Moon (§25a).
- Strive to put **lord of Ascendant with the Lot** (§25b).
- **Never** put Moon in the **2nd, 6th, 8th, or 12th from the Lot** — horrible
  (§25b).

**§26 — Straight vs. crooked Ascendant/Moon; 1st and 4th**

- Always set Ascendant and Moon in signs of **straight ascension** (ease,
  progress); avoid **crooked** (complication, hardship, slowness) (§26).
  ⚠ Dykes notes Carmen V.2 says the **opposite** mapping — preserve both as
  named variants.
- Ascendant and the **fourth sign** from it signify what happens to that
  election (Arabic also: lord of the fourth) (§26).

**§27 — Beginning and end from places**

Look at fortunes and malefics from the **strong and weak places**; speak of
the beginning and end of the matter from that strength and weakness (§27).

## 5. House-by-House Matter Inventory (§§29–143)

Each entry: **significators**, **gates**, **fortifiers**. All inherit §§1–4
unless a chapter overrides. Historical slavery content is flagged.

### 5.1 Second house (§§29–41)

#### §29–31 — Lending money [ELECTION]
- **Prefer Moon in:** Leo, Pisces, Scorpio, Sagittarius, or Aquarius; **defective
  in light**; both fortunes deficient and aspecting Moon or Ascendant (§29a).
- **Fortify:** Mercury cleansed of Mars; Moon with Jupiter or Mercury; fortunes
  not cadent (§29b).
- **Gates:** Moon impeded by malefics; Mercury joined to malefics or in their
  square (§29b). Moon with Mars → labor, worry, harsh business, contention;
  impeded by Saturn → prolonged delay, exit after distress (§29c).
- **Conceal lending (§30):** Moon under rays, going toward conjunction of
  fortunes after separation from Sun → easier, more concealed. Exit from
  combustion toward Mars → made public. Gate: Moon without latitude (Head/Tail)
  or in burnt path (§30c).
- **Dorothean degree gate (§31):** do not lend/take loan while Moon in **first
  degree** of Leo, Gemini, or Sagittarius, or those signs ascending. ⚠ Carmen
  faults degrees only; Sahl Latin extends to whole signs.

#### §32–35 — Business partnerships [ELECTION]
- **Fortify:** Moon cleansed of malefics, joined to fortunes; in **common signs**
  (so multiplied) or in Leo or Taurus (§32a). Received from trine/sextile so
  separation is good (§33).
- **Gates:** Moon in lower (southern-declination) signs — worst Libra (burnt
  path), also Aquarius (§32b). Square/opposition → quarrel in separation (§33).
  Malefics in stakes (§34). Lord of Asc not aspecting Asc, or lord of Moon’s
  house not aspecting Moon → one partner deludes the other (§35).
- **Angular assignment (§34):** Asc = beginner / lesser age; 7th = other
  partner; 10th = what between them / wealth; 4th = conclusion.

#### §36–38 — Investing money for profit [ELECTION]
- **Fortify:** Moon, Mercury, lord of house of assets, lord of degree of house
  of trust (**11th**) (§36). Moon joined to Mercury; Mars cadent from both;
  Mercury purged (§37a). Trust always in Mercury, Moon, degree of house of
  trust, and their lords; Mars and his light cadent from them (§38).
- **If Mercury retrograde:** adapt Moon and degree of house of trust; Mercury
  cadent from Mars’s light but **not** cadent from Venus and lord of 11th
  (§37b).

#### §39–40 — Sales and purchases [ELECTION]
- **Buying (§39):** make Lot of Fortune fit, in houses of Jupiter, joined to
  fortunes → better for buyer. Moon in straight-ascension signs, increased in
  light and number, joined to fortunes → owner of what is bought loses (better
  for seller). Mars cadent from Moon and Mercury; Tail cadent from Moon
  (§39a–d).
- **Selling (§40):** Moon in her exaltation or triplicity, separated from
  fortunes and aspecting malefics, but **not joined** to them (sign-based
  configuration, not degree connection — Dykes).

#### §41 — Alchemical / repeated works [ELECTION]
- Moon in **common signs**, cleansed of malefics; Ascendant likewise. For gold:
  strengthen and fit the Sun (§41).

### 5.3 Fourth house (§§43–55)

#### §43–46 — Building a house [ELECTION]
- **Fortify:** Moon and her lord, Ascendant and its lord, Lot of Fortune,
  Mercury (§43). Moon increased in light and number, joined to Jupiter from
  **square** (better than opposition) → beauty and perfection (§45a). Lords of
  Moon’s house and Asc aspecting them, cleansed of malefics (§46).
- **Gates:** Mars must be cadent from these significators — never give Mars a
  role in building (§44a). If unavoidable: Venus strong over Mars by
  trine/sextile (§44b); Saturn cadent from Venus (§44c). Moon with Saturn or
  Tail, or Saturn in Asc or 4th → slowness, duress, fears, infirmities,
  robbers, death, building splits/falls (§45b–c). Mars aspecting (esp. if
  ascending in apogee circle) → burning/falling feared (§45d). If lords do not
  aspect → owner will not stay (§46).

#### §47 — Destroying a house [ELECTION]
- Moon **descending in her own circle**, separated from malefics, joined to
  fortunes; fortune eastern or ascending direct; or Moon joined to lord of her
  house from esteem (trine/sextile) → easier destruction; square/opposition →
  more difficult (§47).

#### §48–49 — Buying and occupying land [ELECTION]
- Saturn in exaltation, triplicity, or bound; Jupiter aspecting from a stake
  or trine; Mars cadent (§48). Moon at beginning of lunar month, aspecting
  Saturn from esteem, increased in number, in aspect of Jupiter (§49a). If no
  Jupiter–Saturn aspect: Venus instead; make watery signs fortunate (better
  than airy) (§49b). Moon in exaltation or Midheaven; lord of Asc aspecting
  her; Moon and Asc cleansed (§49c).

#### §50 — Digging rivers and wells [ELECTION]
- Saturn eastern; Moon under earth in 3rd or 5th, free from malefics,
  fortunate and received (§50a). Gate: malefic in Midheaven → well tumbles /
  river runs dry (§50b). Prefer Saturn in 11th; Moon joined to fortune in fixed
  sign, fortune ascending in circle (§50c). Best fortune = Jupiter; else Jupiter
  in Midheaven for lasting river/stable well (§50d).

#### §51–53 — Planting trees [ELECTION]
- Moon in **fixed** sign; lord of her house aspecting from watery signs (§51).
  Ascendant fixed or common; lord of Asc **ascending and eastern** (§52a).
  Tempo matrix (§52b–d): ascending not eastern → sprout fast, fruit delayed;
  eastern descending → sprout slow, fruit fast; eastern ascending → both fast;
  western descending → both slow. Lords aspecting, free of malefics and
  burning (§53).

#### §54–55 — Sowing seed [ELECTION]
- Ascendant **common**; its lord in **movable** sign, aspecting lord of its own
  house, free from malefics (§54). Moon increased in light and number (§55a).
  Gate: Moon under rays and defective in number → seed vanishes (§55a).

### 5.4 Fifth house (§§56–59)

#### §56–57 — Conceiving a child (sex for generation) [ELECTION]
- **Male child:** Asc and its lord, Moon and lord of house of children, in
  **masculine** signs or masculine part of circle; only masculine planet in Asc
  or sign of children (§56).
- **Female:** same significators in **feminine** signs / feminine part (§57a).
- If mixed: lord of hour and planet receiving Moon’s disposition partner with
  those having more testimony in the desired gender (§57b).

#### §58 — Miscarriage / extracting dead fetus [ELECTION — obstetric]
- Moon defective in light, descending from the belt toward the south, aspecting
  fortunes from trine or square **with aspect of Mars** (§58a). Prefer Moon and
  Asc in feminine signs of **straight** ascension, not crooked (§58b). Cf.
  Carmen V.18.

#### §59 — Educating children / training / profession [ELECTION]
- Moon aspecting Mercury; both free from malefics (§59a). Ascendant Gemini or
  Virgo; Mercury eastern, ascending — not descending, retrograde, first
  station, descension, or impeded; lord of Mercury’s house likewise (§59b).
  Gate: Moon descending and deficient in light → training slows (§59c).

### 5.5 Sixth house (§§60–77)

#### §60 — Exorcisms / removing infestation [ELECTION]
- **Gate signs for Moon or Asc:** Leo, Cancer, Scorpio, Aquarius (§60b).
- Prefer Moon in other signs, separated from malefics, joined to fortunes
  (§60b). Cf. Carmen V.37.

#### §61–63 — Medicines by body region (scheme 1) [ELECTION — medical]
- **Bowels / belly / plaster (§61):** Moon or Asc in Libra or Scorpio (Moon in
  it), joined to fortunes; no malefic in stakes of the Moon. If unavoidable:
  trine/sextile only — not opposition, besieging rays, or under Sun’s rays.
- **Head / gargling / vomiting (§62a):** Asc and Moon in Aries or Taurus; Moon
  defective in light, joined to fortunes. Gate: Sun square/opposition in Aries
  especially.
- **Nostrils / fumigations / sneezing-powders (§62b):** Cancer, Leo, or Virgo
  ascending; Moon joined to fortunes — not to malefics, retrograde, or impeded
  planets.
- **Hands and feet (§63):** Capricorn, Aquarius, or Pisces ascending; Moon in
  them, joined to fortunes.

#### §64 — Curing old diseases [ELECTION — medical]
- Moon in her triplicity (better **Taurus**, earthy) (§64a). Cleansed of
  malefics; fortunes in stakes of the Moon from Taurus. Gate: Moon joined to
  Saturn → prolongs illness (§64b).

#### §65 — Māshāʾallāh melothesia by Moon-sign triads [ELECTION — medical]
| Body region | Moon in |
|---|---|
| Head, throat, chest (upper) | Aries, Taurus, Gemini (§65a) |
| Belly, pubic, navel (middle) | Cancer, Leo, Virgo (§65b) |
| Anus, lower body | Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius (§65c) |
| Knees to feet | Capricorn, Aquarius, Pisces (§65d) |

Moon joined to fortunes, increased in light and number.

#### §66 — Circle upper/lower cure (Māshāʾallāh / Dorotheus) [ELECTION — medical]
- Head to navel: Moon between stake of earth ascending to Midheaven (“upper
  part of circle”) (§66a).
- Navel to feet: Moon between tenth descending to stake of earth (“lower”)
  (§66b).
- Fortune in Ascendant → healed and progresses (§66c).

#### §67–69 — Eyes, iron, cupping, bloodletting, teeth [ELECTION — medical]
- **Iron / scarify / cut vein (§67a–d):** Moon increased in light and number —

### 5.6 Seventh house (§§78–96)

#### §78–84 — Marriage / engagement / sexual joining [ELECTION]
- **Engagement (§78–79a):** Gate Moon in 12th; avoid Aries, Cancer, Capricorn,

  **movable** sign — best **Libra**. Beware fixed signs for engagement.

  - Mid-Taurus better than beginning/end; first half Gemini worse, end good;
  - Aries & Cancer bad; Leo praiseworthy but partners destroy each other’s assets;
  - Virgo useful for previously married woman, not virgin; Libra bad;
  - Beginning Scorpio useful, end bad (partnership not prolonged);
  - Sagittarius bad; beginning Capricorn bad, mid/end good; Aquarius & Pisces bad.
- **Venus (§81):** no use if Venus aspects malefics. Prefer Venus in houses/
  bounds of fortunes, joined to lord of her house; if that lord malefic,
  separate and let Jupiter overcome her or trine. Moon–Jupiter–Venus mutual
  trine/sextile (trine best, esp. watery triplicity). Moon increased, free of
  malefics; Venus in house/exaltation/triplicity/joy or with Jupiter/Mercury;
  Mercury fortunate (§81d).
- **Gender significators (§82):** Sun + Asc = man; Venus + Moon + 7th = woman.
  Gate malefics by conjunction/square/opposition.
- **Woman marrying (§83):** Moon in common signs; no malefic in Asc or aspecting
  from enmity; fortune in Midheaven. ⚠ §83b–84 Midheaven fortune / watery MC
  pregnancy rule is **synastry/natal-overlay** (Dorotheus V.17), not pure
  election.

  7th, 8th; not burned, cadent, or joined to unreceptive cadent planet (§86a).

- To force encounter: Mars in stakes; fortune with role in Asc joined to Mars
  to bar him from Asc (§87a). Go to war only if Mars in friendship aspect to
  lord of Asc (or is lord of Asc), strong, good place, not impeded/burned, in
  straight-ascension signs; Mars in **domain (hayyiz)** of Asc (§87b–c). ⚠
  hayyiz = sect/rejoicing or eastern hemisphere — ambiguous.
- 2nd + lord = initiator’s soldiers; 8th + lord = enemy’s; do not put lord of
  8th in 7th or 8th — put in 2nd (§88a). Lot of Fortune + lord in Asc or 2nd,
  not 8th/7th (§88b). Fit Mars, Mercury, Moon, lord of Moon’s house; dignity of
  twelfth-part of Moon (§88c–89).
- **Natal overlay (§90):** of two wisely elected armies, victory to him born at
  night with Mars having a role in the nativity. ⚠ Requires natal input.

### 5.8 Ninth house (§§101–122)

#### §101–107 — Foreign travel in general [ELECTION + rootedness]

  itself (§102b).
- Do not make Asc of question and its lord cadent from Asc of departure
  (§103a). Asc of departure = 10th from question/nativity Asc if seeking
  kingdom; = 11th if business; likewise map sought house to election Asc

- Moon in stakes/succeedents if free of malefics, aspecting Asc; if impeded,
  cadent from Asc (§104a). Lords of Asc and Moon’s house in stakes; Moon
  aspects her lord (§104b).
- **Gates:** Moon with malefics or their square/opposition — malefic aspect to
  Asc easier than to Moon, esp. in travel; Moon+Mars at month-beginning →
  robbers, tyrant, fire (§105). Never Moon in 4th; prefer 5th if fortunate
  (§106). Moon in Asc at entrance/departure → infirmity or heavy labor feared
  (§107).

#### §114–115 — Sea travel [ELECTION]
- Moon in watery signs; beware Moon–Saturn conjunction from a stake; Saturn not
  in watery sign, not fixed on Asc of departure or with Moon (§114a). If

- Luminaries not impeded; free of malefics (Carmen: also free of fortunes —

- Business sea travel: adapt Mercury and Moon; aspect Jupiter from Cancer or

#### §116–117 — Land vs sea [ELECTION]
- Land: Moon not in watery signs, cleansed of malefics; beware Mars (as Saturn

  without Jupiter aspect (§117a).

- Adapt Asc, its lord, Moon, lord of 2nd; make lord of 2nd a fortune above

  whether fortune or malefic.
- Lord of Moon’s house with lord of 2nd above earth (under earth only for

- Concealment: Moon between **12° and 15°** from Sun; better if fortunate in
  first 3° exiting rays (§120b).
- Seeking kingdom in region: fit MC and its lord with 2nd and Moon (§121).

  aspecting 4th and MC (§126b–c).

  fortunate in good place from Asc (stronger for your matters) (§130). ⚠ Bracket

  or its triplicity (whole-sign trine) or square — beware opposition; Moon not
  joined to malefics; must aspect lord of her house or matter not perfected
  (§132).

  fortunes (direct + increased fortunes → increased result) (§132c).
- Gate: Mercury impeded even if received → inconvenience, duress, second return

- Join Moon to planet of the matter: Sun for king, Mars for generals, etc.

### 5.11 Twelfth house (§§135–142)

#### §135–137 — Buying animals [ELECTION]
- Moon joined to fortunes direct, eastern, ascending; beware malefic conjunction
  (§135).
- **Tamed/ridden (§136):** Asc common, Moon in fixed (except Aquarius, Scorpio).
  Planet joined: direct ascending → price and body increase; retrograde
  ascending → body diminish, price increase; direct descending → body increase,
  price not appropriate.
- **Untamed (§137):** Asc common, Moon in movable, joined to fortune; then same
  ascending/descending criteria as §135.

#### §138–140 — Hunting and fishing [ELECTION]
- Go out under common sign; lord of 7th defective/decreasing and descending, in
  succeedents (cadent → prey escapes after catch) (§138a).
- Moon separated from Mars, fortunate in optimal place from Asc; gates: Moon at
  end of signs, VOC, or movable; lord of Moon’s house should aspect her
  (§138b–c).
- Water hunt: Mercury cleansed of malefics. Mountain hunt: Moon in Aries and
  its triplicity. Bird hunt: Moon in Gemini and its triplicity, joined to or
  separated from Mercury descending (§139).
- Sea hunt: Asc common, lord in watery sign; Asc not fiery; Moon aspecting her
  lord. Mars impediment worse at sea; Saturn worse on land. Venus+Moon fit,
  Mars not impeding → catch doubled; Moon joined to Venus with Mercury; Saturn
  impeding Mercury does not impede this. Gate: Mars in watery sign with Moon or
  Venus joined to Mars (§140).

#### §141 — Fleeing / concealed actions [ELECTION]
- Moon separated from malefics, joined to fortunes; Moon joined to Saturn under
  rays, then joined to fortunes at exit from under rays (§141a). ⚠ Crofts
  applies exit clause to Saturn.
- Beware luminary aspects — luminaries uncover and make appear (§141b).

#### §142 — Pursuing fugitives [MIXED / HORARY-shaped]
- Moon joined to malefics, or exiting under rays and then encountering a malefic
  by square/opposition/conjunction ⚠ (Arabic omits conjunction); never put Moon
  or planetary receiver of disposition in the 4th (§142). Cf. Sahl *On
  Questions* / Judges — interrogation lineage.

### 5.12 Outside the twelve signs (§143)

#### §143 — Writing a letter [ELECTION]
- Moon joined with Mercury, cleansed of malefics; Mercury strong and fortunate,
  not retrograde nor impeded; both Moon and Mercury cleansed of malefics
  (§143).
