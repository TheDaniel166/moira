## 0. Provenance and Scope

**Source (primary, in hand):** Dorotheus of Sidon, *Carmen Astrologicum*, the
ʿUmar al-Tabarī translation (Arabic), **2nd edition**, translated & edited by
Benjamin N. Dykes, PhD (Cazimi Press). **Book V — "On Questions or
Inceptions," pp. 231–330** (PDF held on disk). Cross-references to Hephaistion
of Thebes (*Apotelesmatika* III), the Dorotheus Greek Fragments/Excerpts, and
Theophilus of Edessa are Dykes' apparatus and are noted where they clarify a
rule.

**Citation convention:** `V.ch, sent` = Book V, chapter, sentence (Dykes
numbering). Where a rule rests on Hephaistion's parallel rather than the Arabic,
it is marked *(Heph.)*.

**Confidence:** every parameter here is **[V+, primary text]** in the sense that
it is stated in the named authority at the cited page. V.6,3-15 was separately
rendered and visually re-confirmed before the bounded profile was admitted.
Disputed readings remain marked and are not converted into executable truth.

**What Dorotheus is.** Book V is the oldest surviving electional treatise. Its
architecture is explicit and three-layered, and *every* matter-specific chapter
inherits the two general layers below it:

1. **Sign-nature layer** (V.2–V.5): the ascending sign and the Moon's sign
   condition the *tempo and shape* of the outcome (fast/slow, stable/breaking,
   single/doubled, day/night fit).
2. **Moon-corruption layer** (V.6): a checklist of lunar impediments that
   forbid or spoil an inception, plus the root/outcome doctrine (Moon = root,
   lord of Moon's sign = outcome) and the separation/connection doctrine.
3. **Significator-of-the-matter layer** (V.31): each kind of business has a
   "lord of the work" (a planet and one or more houses) that must also be
   fortified.

**Election vs. horary.** Book V mixes true *inceptions* (elections — you choose
the moment) with *interrogations* (horary — you read the moment a question or
event occurred). The horary chapters cannot be used as electional gates and are
flagged as **[HORARY — out of electional scope]** in §3. The electionally
usable chapters are the focus of this inventory.

## 1. General Layer A — Sign Nature (V.2–V.5)

These condition the **shape and tempo** of any elected matter. They are
scorers/annotators, not hard gates (Dorotheus softens each with the fortunes).

### 1.1 Crooked vs. straight signs — ascensional (V.2)

Signs of **long ascension** ("straight in arising," northern hemisphere) make a
matter **easy and fast**; signs of **short ascension** ("crooked") make it
**hard, slow, toilsome**.

| Class | Signs (Dorotheus, N. hemisphere) | Effect on the work |
|---|---|---|
| Straight (long ascension) | Cancer, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius | easy, fast (V.2,3) |
| Crooked (short ascension) | Capricorn, Aquarius, Pisces, Aries, Taurus, Gemini | difficult, slow (V.2,4·6) |

- Crooked Ascendant + a fortune in it or aspecting it → burden removed (V.2,7).
- Straight Ascendant + an infortune in it or aspecting it → delay, unrest, toil
  (V.2,8).
- Fortunes **and** infortunes both on/aspecting the Ascendant → middling,
  mixed outcome (V.2,9).

> **Engine note.** These are *hemisphere-* and *latitude-dependent* (they are
> oblique-ascension classes, not fixed sign lists). Moira must compute them from
> ascensional times at the election's latitude, **not** hardcode the sign list —
> that is exactly the kind of table-era shortcut Part V.4 forbids. The governing
> object is "sign of long vs. short ascension at this geographic latitude."

### 1.2 Convertible (cardinal) Ascendant (V.3)

A **convertible/movable** rising sign → the result **breaks off before
conclusion**, and the matter will have to be begun again. Gate-worthy for any
matter wanting permanence.

### 1.3 Twin (bicorporeal) Ascendant (V.4)

A **twin/double-bodied** rising sign → the work is not completed until a
*second* matter enters, which finishes before the first; i.e. the matter
**doubles**. Relevant to repeat-transaction and "will it be singular?" elections.

### 1.4 Diurnal / nocturnal fit (V.5)

Day-begun work is strongest with a **diurnal Ascendant + Moon in a diurnal
sign**; night-begun work with a **nocturnal Ascendant + Moon in a nocturnal
sign** (V.5,4). Explicitly emphasized **for travel and boarding ships**.

| Sect | Triplicities | Signs |
|---|---|---|
| Diurnal | Aries-tripl. + Gemini-tripl. | Aries, Leo, Sagittarius, Gemini, Libra, Aquarius |
| Nocturnal | Taurus-tripl. + Cancer-tripl. | Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn, Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces |

## 2. General Layer B — The Corruption of the Moon (V.6) + the Root/Outcome and Separation/Connection Doctrines

This is the single most important electional chapter: the **lunar impediment
checklist** that every matter chapter refers back to ("the Moon cleansed of the
misfortunes I described at the beginning of my book"). It is the Dorothean
ancestor of the later "ten impediments of the Moon" and of void-of-course.

### 2.1 The impediment checklist (V.6, 3–14) — gates

**Page-confirmation record (2026-07-14/15):** V.6,3–15 was rendered and visually
checked against the held Dykes ʿUmar al-Ṭabarī PDF, printed pp. 233–235 (PDF
pp. 251–253). The check confirms that under-rays (V.6,4), least motion below
12°/day (V.6,11), and the burned path (V.6,12) are three separate rules. It
does not resolve the latitude/node wording in V.6,7 or narrow Dorotheus's
“Libra and Scorpio” to the later 15°–15° interval.

Do not begin an inception when any of these hold (each is a distinct condition —
**preserve them as named variants; do not merge**):

| # | Impediment | Parameter / definition | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Moon eclipsed | at/near an eclipse | harsher if in the natal Moon's sign or its trine (V.6,3) |
| 2 | Moon under the rays / combust | "annihilated by his glow, not seen" | **but GOOD for concealed works** — theft, betrayal, secrets (V.6,4); best begun as she disengages and reappears (V.6,5) |
| 3 | Moon in the twelfth-part of Mars or Saturn | Moon's 2.5° dodecatemorion falls in a traditional domicile of Mars or Saturn (V.6,6) | admitted explicit twelfth-part computation |
| 4 | Moon on the ecliptic, descending south | "middle of the line of equality, descending toward the south" (V.6,7) | ⚠ edition note 15 identifies the ecliptic but supplies no crossing region or tolerance; v1 is `not_evaluable` |
| 5 | Moon in opposition to the Sun (Full) | whole-sign opposition under the edition glossary | bad; indicates lawsuits, "the younger will be defeated" (V.6,8) |
| 6 | Moon conjunct or aspecting an infortune | with, or looking at, Mars/Saturn (V.6,9) | |
| 7 | Moon disengaging from the Sun in longitude or latitude | measured separation and latitude are preserved, but the text gives no connection interval or latitude criterion (V.6,10) | v1 is `not_evaluable`; no just-separated-New-Moon proxy is asserted |
| 8 | **Moon slow / least motion** | **daily motion < 12°/day** → "resembles Saturn"; brings difficulty & slowness (V.6,11) | clause 11 itself defines the least-motion comparison; no acceleration derivative is added |
| 9 | Moon in the burned path (via combusta) | "the middle of the line of equality — **Libra and Scorpio**" (V.6,12) | Dorothean via combusta = whole Libra+Scorpio ⚠ (later authorities narrow to 15°Lib–15°Sco) |
| 10 | Moon in the last degrees of a sign | terminal Egyptian bound ending at 30°, ruled by Saturn or Mars (V.6,13) | Dykes introduction p. 36 identifies Dorotheus's bound use as Egyptian |
| 11 | Moon cadent from the 10th toward the 9th, in a "double place" | explicit quadrant ninth-place fall from the Midheaven → work nullified, unstable (V.6,14) | not generic 3/6/9/12 cadency |

**Remedy when the election cannot be postponed (V.6,15):** place **Jupiter or
Venus in the Ascendant or the Midheaven.**

### 2.2 Planetary condition (V.6, 16–18) — general

- A planet **under the rays, retrograde, or cadent** ("falling from a stake,
  double place") has little power (V.6,16).
- A planet **with an infortune in the same degree**, or aspected by one
  (trine/square/sextile), or **in the same sign** as one → the work "will not
  have power" (V.6,17).
- Planets in the signs the scholars called **"dark"** → neither they nor their
  rays have power (V.6,18). *(The "dark/smoky/void/bright degrees" table is
  referenced but not given here — must be sourced separately if used.)*

### 2.3 Root and outcome — the Moon-and-her-lord doctrine (V.6, 19–26, attributed to Valens)

Look at the **Sun, the Moon, the lord of each luminary's sign, plus the
Ascendant and Midheaven** (V.6,20). Then:

- **The Moon = indicator of the *root* of every work; the lord of the Moon's
  sign = indicator of its *outcome*** (V.6,22). This is the governing rule.

| Moon (root) | Lord of Moon's sign (outcome) | Result |
|---|---|---|
| good place | bad/cadent | good start, bad end (V.6,21) |
| bad place | strong place | bad start, good end (V.6,22) |
| strong | strong | both good (V.6,24) |
| double/cadent | double/cadent | both bad, end worse than start (V.6,25) |
| — | in the signs *following* the stakes (succedent) | delayed outcome (V.6,26) |

### 2.4 Separation and connection (V.6, 27–31)

- **Moon separating from a fortune** → good for most works (V.6,28) — **EXCEPT**
  fleeing authority/land, escaping a master, theft: those want the Moon
  **separating from an infortune** (V.6,28).
- Examine the editorial **lord of the ninth-part**, the **lord of the Lot of
  Fortune** for the *inception*, and **whom the Moon connects with next** for
  the *outcome* (V.6,29). Edition note 31 identifies the ninth-parts phrase as
  a Persian editorial insertion and supplies no division/boundary scheme;
  Moira therefore exposes it as `not_evaluable`. Fortune is evaluated through
  the existing sect-aware Lots engine, and the next connection through the
  sign-bounded exact search. These supplement rather than replace the V.6.22
  Moon-sign lord.
- Summary (V.6,31): read the Moon, the lord of her sign, and the planet she
  next connects with, and the strength of each relative to the angles.

### 2.5 Besieging and rescue; the Moon's placement (V.6, 32–35)

- Moon **besieged** between the two infortunes in one sign, but the fortunes
  square her → rescue from misfortune (V.6,32).
- Moon **strongest above the earth** (especially by night); the **Ascendant
  strongest when the Moon is under the earth by day** (V.6,35).

## 3. General Layer C — The Significator of the Matter (V.31)

Every inception also has a **"lord of the work."** Fortify it (not under the
rays, not made unfortunate, aspecting the Ascendant, in a good place) in
addition to the Moon and Ascendant (V.31,2). Moira preserves those four items
as separate testimonies. “Made unfortunate” remains a typed unresolved source
gate because the passage does not define an exclusive predicate set.

| Matter | Lord of the work (planet) | (Houses, from the matter chapters) |
|---|---|---|
| Buying land, estate management | Saturn **and** Jupiter (V.31,3) | 4th (the land) |
| Taking, gifts, lawsuits, involvement, partnership, close relationships, friendship, business, seeking culture | **Mercury** (V.31,4) | per chapter |
| Marriage, sex, pleasant Venusian works | **Venus** (V.31,5) | 7th |
| War, arms | **Mars** (V.31,6) | — |
| Matters of the Sultan / kings / petitions before kings | **Jupiter** (V.31,7) | 10th |
| Manifest, non-secret, prominent matters | **the Sun** (with Jupiter) (V.31,8) | — |

- **Jupiter aspecting any planet directs it toward good** (V.31,8); Venus does
  likewise but weakly, "not in the most significant matters unless Jupiter is
  with her or aspects her" (V.31,9). Jupiter is universally suitable (V.31,11).

## 4. Matter-Specific Chapters (V.7–V.44)

Each entry: **significators** (houses/planets the chapter assigns), **gates**
(avoid), **fortifiers** (prefer), and **sign tables** where the chapter gives
one. All inherit §1–§3 unless a chapter overrides.

### V.7 — Building a building (construction) [ELECTION]
- **Fortify:** Moon **increasing in light and calculation**, on the ecliptic
  rising toward the **north**, with **Jupiter or Venus** conjunct or aspecting
  her from a strong place (V.7,1).
- **Gates:** **Saturn** with/aspecting the Moon → difficulty, delay, toil
  (V.7,2). **Mars** with/aspecting the Moon → fire / conflagration damage to the
  building (V.7,3).

### V.8 — Demolishing a building [ELECTION]
- **Fortify:** begin as the **Moon descends from her height (toward her low
  point / southward)** — the inverse of V.7 (V.8,1).
- Fortunes → easy success; infortunes → slow, hard (V.8,2).

### V.9 — Leasing / hiring [ELECTION, angular-significator]
- **Houses:** Ascendant = the hirer (party with money/land needing work); 7th =
  the owner of the thing/labor hired; 10th = the amount/price; 4th = the outcome
  (V.9,1).
- **Gates:** infortune in the **1st** → the hirer backs out or acts deceitfully
  (V.9,2–3); in the **7th** → the owner backs out / betrays (V.9,4–5); in the
  **10th** → the price/amount goes wrong (V.9,6); in the **4th** → bad outcome
  even if arranged (V.9,7).
- The Moon's separation and connection also signify (V.9,8).

### V.10 — Buying and selling [ELECTION, dual method]
- **Method 1 (lunar):** the planet the **Moon connects with** = the buyer & the
  price; the planet she **separates from** = the seller; the **Moon herself** =
  the commodity (V.10,1). Infortunes on any of these → trouble to that party;
  fortunes → success (V.10,3–4).
- **Method 2 (angular):** 1st = buyer, 7th = seller, 10th = price, 4th =
  commodity (V.10,6). Fortunes/infortunes on a house help/harm that party
  (V.10,7).

### V.11 — Buying land [ELECTION, angular-significator]
- **Houses:** 4th = the land itself; 10th = what is on it (trees); 7th = ground
  cover (grasses/vegetation); 1st = the cultivation (V.11,1). 4th in a watery
  sign → land near water; in a twin sign → mixed terrain (V.11,2).

### V.12 — Buying a slave [MOON-IN-SIGN table] ⚠ *(historical content; the astrological mechanic is Moon-sign → nature of acquisition)*
- Governed by the **sign of the Moon** (and, secondarily, the Ascendant,
  V.12,13) when fortunes/infortunes do not aspect (V.12,14). A 12-sign
  character/aptitude table (V.12,1–12). Structurally: **Moon-sign → the innate
  quality of the thing acquired.** *(Modern reuse would be as a "nature of the
  acquired thing" annotator, not as a person-election.)*

### V.13 — Buying an animal [ELECTION, Moon/Asc-in-sign]
- **Tamed animal:** Ascendant **Aries** (Moon in Aries preferable), or Ascendant
  **Taurus with the Moon in it** (V.13,1–2).
- **Untamed animal:** Ascendant (and Moon) in the **last degrees of Leo** or the
  **last degrees of Sagittarius** (the bestial halves) (V.13,3).

### V.14 — Emancipating a slave [ELECTION, angular + lunar] *(historical)*
- Moon **cleansed** of the V.6 impediments (V.14,1). **Moon separating from
  infortunes and connecting to fortunes** → good for the freed person; the
  reverse → bondage was better than the freedom he gets (V.14,2–3).
- **Houses:** 1st = master, 7th = the freed slave, 10th = the reason, 4th =
  outcome (V.14,5). Structurally a clean **separation→connection = trajectory**
  election.

### V.15 — Seeking a need or a gift from a ruler/man [ELECTION]
- **Fortify:** Moon in the **1st, or the square or trine of it**, **increasing
  in light**; the lord of the Ascendant **direct** (not retrograde), conjunct
  the Moon and the Moon connecting to it (V.15,1). Better if the Moon is in her
  own house or aspects it, and **Mercury is with Jupiter** (V.15,2).
- **Gates:** Mercury with/aspected by **Saturn** → no good word, request fails
  (V.15,3). Petitioning **kings/nobles:** worse if **Jupiter is retrograde** or
  **Saturn aspects Jupiter** (V.15,5).
- **Audience-tuned significator** (extends V.31): before **women** → Mercury
  with Venus (V.15,4); before **merchants/scholars** → Mercury strong (V.15,6);
  before **elders / the childless / the criminal / slaves** → Saturn strong
  (V.15,7).

### V.16 — Writing to a man / teaching knowledge or writing [ELECTION]
- **Fortify:** **Mercury with the Moon**, no infortune conjunct/aspecting her;
  **Mercury eastern (oriental), not under the rays, not retrograde**; Moon
  cleansed of the V.6 impediments (V.16,1).

### V.17 — Marriage and sex [ELECTION, rich] — 7th/Venus/Moon
- **Significators (V.17,1):** the man = **Sun + Ascendant + the planet the Moon
  separates from**; the woman = **Venus + 7th + the planet the Moon connects
  to**; 10th = what passes between them; 4th = outcome & dowry.
- **Gates:**
  - Sun afflicted while Venus is with/aspected by Jupiter → marriage short for
    the man, good for the woman (V.17,2).
  - Venus with **Mars or Saturn** (or aspected) → trouble, exposure of faults
    for the woman, quick separation (V.17,3).
  - **Moon afflicted** → evil to both (V.17,4).
  - **Moon (or Moon & Venus) in a convertible sign** → not suitable; no harmony,
    no lasting companionship; (Moon+Venus convertible → the woman a harlot)
    (V.17,6–7).
  - Anything of the infortunes with the Moon or in the Ascendant → no agreement,
    estrangement (V.17,36).
- **Fortifiers:**
  - **Mercury with the fortunes** → children born quickly (V.17,5).
  - **Venus in a fortune's house with Jupiter aspecting Venus and the Moon**
    (V.17,21); Moon, Jupiter, Venus in mutual trine, in fertile signs → suitable
    & fertile (V.17,23).
  - Strongest overall if the **Moon is increasing in light and calculation**
    (V.17,35).
- **Moon-in-sign marriage table (V.17,9–20):** a 12-sign suitability list
  (Aries not good; Taurus only mid-sign; Gemini only 2nd half; Cancer avoid; Leo
  ok but wasteful; Virgo widow-yes/virgin-no; Libra engagement-yes/marriage-no;
  Scorpio 1st degree yes, end no; Sagittarius delay; Capricorn mid/end yes;
  Aquarius no; Pisces "woman corrupted" but ok for other things). **Preserve as
  a named Dorothean Moon-sign marriage table.**
- **Natal-overlay rules (V.17,25–34, 37–39):** fertility and affection read from
  *both natal charts* (a fortune in both 10ths → children that year; a shared
  fortune-sign → mutual love; opposed natal Moons → estrangement). **These are
  synastry/rootedness rules — they require natal input** (see §5, radicality).

### V.18 — Betrothal / a wife who has left home, will she return [MIXED: election + horary]
- Reads **Sun + Venus** positions (Sun above earth in 10th/11th + **Venus
  retrograde, western** → she returns and stays obedient; Venus direct → she
  returns forceful, estrangement) (V.18,2–6). **Moon leaving the opposition of
  the Sun** → she returns without difficulty (V.18,8). Largely **horary** in
  framing (reading the moment of departure); the electional residue is the
  Venus/Moon-phase symbolism.

### V.19 — Extracting a dead fetus [ELECTION — obstetric surgery]
- **Fortify:** **Moon waning in light**, descending toward the south (in
  latitude), with **Mars and Venus** aspecting the Ascendant and Moon by square
  & trine; preferable if Ascendant & Moon are in a **feminine sign** and in
  **signs of straight ascension** (V.19,1–2). *(A companion to the surgery
  chapter V.40; note Mars is here a positive cutting-significator.)*

### V.20 — Entering a partnership [ELECTION, Moon-in-sign] — Mercury/2nd
- Moon in a **marriage-suitable sign** (per V.17) and **cleansed** of the V.6
  impediments (V.20,1).
- **Moon-in-sign partnership table (V.20,2–13):** Aries no (won't last); Taurus
  qualified; Gemini good/faithful; Cancer betrayal; Leo good; **Virgo excellent
  (profit, esteem)**; Libra no; Scorpio conflict/concealed malice; Sagittarius
  ok but mutual arrogance; Capricorn joy; Aquarius harm; **Pisces excellent.**
- **Modifiers:** **Saturn** on Asc/Moon → chilled affection, dissent, delay
  (V.20,15); **Mars** → conflict, fighting (V.20,16); **Jupiter from trine** →
  benefit & esteem (V.20,17); any fortune from trine best, from square/sextile
  weaker (V.20,18–19).

### V.21 — Debt and its payment [ELECTION, angular + lunar] — Moon/Mercury
- **Significators:** 1st = the creditor (who owns the debt); 7th = the debtor;
  Moon & Mercury also = the debtor (V.21,1).
- **Gates:** Moon with fortunes but also afflicted → he pays but re-incurs
  (V.21,2); **Mercury with Saturn** → confusion/deception in payment (V.21,3);
  **Mercury with/aspecting Mars** → default, fighting over the debt (V.21,4).
- If the Moon is under the rays, judge from the **Sun** instead (V.21,5).
- **Gate (via combusta / sign list):** do **not** lend/incur when the Moon is in
  the **burned path**, or in the **first degrees of Leo, Gemini, or
  Sagittarius**, or those are the Ascendant (V.21,6). **Prefer:** Moon in **Leo,
  Pisces, Aquarius, Scorpio, or Sagittarius**, **decreasing in light**, with
  **Jupiter, Venus, and Mercury** aspecting the Ascendant or Moon (best if
  simultaneously) (V.21,7–8).

### V.22 — Travel / departure [ELECTION] — Moon/1st/7th
- **Significators:** 1st = traveler; 7th = destination land; 10th = the business
  sought; 4th = outcome (V.22,1).
- **Fortify:** **Moon increasing in calculation**; **Mercury not under the rays
  nor with infortunes**; **Moon not in the 6th or 12th** from the Ascendant;
  lord of Asc and lord of Moon not under-rays/cadent (V.22,2). Moon in the 6th
  or 12th → toil, harm on the journey (V.22,3).
- Fortunes in 1st → journey turns good; in 7th → benefit at destination
  (V.22,5–6). **Moon + Saturn/Mars in the 1st or 7th** → pain, illness, loss,
  strife on the road (V.22,7). Lord of Asc/Moon **stationary** → journey
  prolonged (V.22,8). Infortune squaring/opposing the Moon (and she connects to
  it) → bad journey (V.22,9–10).

### V.23 — Turning back from the journey / the return [MIXED: election + event-reading]
- Reads the **opposition and square of the Sun**, the infortunes' positions, and
  the **lord of the 2nd** at the destination to time and characterize the
  return (V.23). Also: **Mars in Sagittarius with/aspecting the Moon** → beating,
  wounding on the road (V.23,16); essence of the Moon's afflicting sign →
  danger from beasts vs. humans (V.23,17–19). Largely a *reading of an existing
  departure chart*; the pure electional residue is "avoid Moon afflicted by
  malefics at departure."

### V.24 — Buying / commissioning a ship [ELECTION] — 4th/Jupiter/Venus
- **Fortify:** **Jupiter or Venus in the 4th**, aspecting Ascendant and Moon
  (V.24,1), preferably in a **watery / marine sign** or a Jupiter/Venus house
  (V.24,2). **Best Ascendants (with the Moon there), ranked:** **Taurus > Pisces
  > Gemini > end of Capricorn**, also Cancer, Virgo, Sagittarius (V.24,3–5).

### V.25 — Building (constructing) a ship [ELECTION]
- **Fortify:** **Sun in trine of the fortunes**; **Moon increasing in light,
  calculation, latitude, and longitude**, in a **bound of the fortunes**
  (V.25,1).
- **Gate:** Moon & Ascendant afflicted by **Mars** → outcome ruined by
  fire/anger (V.25,2). Detailed Moon-sign + Sun/Mars-affliction damage table
  (Aquarius → fire/jettison; marine sign → sinking; dry sign → wreck on
  rocks; etc.) (V.25,3–6).

### V.26 — Launching a ship ("pushing into the water") [ELECTION, Moon-in-sign + Saturn doctrine]
- **Moon-in-sign launch table (V.26,1–13)**, read when fortunes/infortunes do
  not aspect the Moon (e.g. **first 10° Aries above earth** → good departure;
  Taurus → a survivable surge; Cancer → safe; Libra 0–10° → bad by land & sea;
  etc.).
- **Moon above/under the earth + Saturn doctrine (V.26,14–33):** Moon **under
  the earth** with fortunes/infortunes aspecting → bad; **Saturn stationary with
  the Moon under the earth** → the ship floods, crew lost; **Jupiter (with
  Venus) aspecting the Moon above the earth** → safe, prosperous voyage. A rich
  malefic-station + hemisphere doctrine.
- **Timing sub-elections (V.26,37):** the chart for **loosing the ship** vs. for
  **a passenger boarding** (foot on deck) are distinct inceptions.
- **Dry/wet-sign travel rule (V.26,39–43):** for **land** travel avoid the Moon
  in the 7th in a non-dry sign with infortunes in dry signs; for **sea** travel
  avoid the Moon not in a watery sign with infortunes there. **Mars is the worst
  for land travel, Saturn for sea travel** (V.26,42); worst of all when Jupiter
